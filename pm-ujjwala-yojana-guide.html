Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana

Providing clean cooking fuel to women from poor households across India

1. What is PM Ujjwala Yojana?

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a flagship scheme launched by the Government of India in May 2016 under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas.

The scheme aims to provide deposit-free LPG connections to adult women belonging to poor households so that families can shift from traditional cooking fuels to cleaner cooking energy.

Main Objective: To make clean cooking fuel accessible to economically weaker households and reduce dependence on traditional fuels such as firewood, coal and dung cakes.

2. Launch and Development

  • 1 May 2016: PMUY was launched at Ballia, Uttar Pradesh.
  • 2019: The original target of 8 crore LPG connections was achieved.
  • August 2021: Ujjwala 2.0 was launched with additional connections and special provisions for migrant families.
  • December 2022: The target of 1.6 crore additional Ujjwala 2.0 connections was achieved.
  • 2023–24: An additional allocation of 75 lakh connections was approved and achieved in July 2024.
  • 2025–26: An additional 25 lakh connections were approved.
PMUY has expanded in stages to reach more eligible households across the country.

3. Objectives of the Scheme

  • Provide clean cooking fuel to poor households.
  • Reduce dependence on firewood, coal and other traditional fuels.
  • Reduce indoor air pollution caused by traditional cooking methods.
  • Improve the health and living conditions of women and families.
  • Reduce the time and effort spent collecting cooking fuel.
  • Promote cleaner household energy use.
  • Support environmental conservation.

4. What is LPG?

LPG stands for Liquefied Petroleum Gas. It is a fuel commonly used for cooking in homes and commercial kitchens.

LPG is stored in pressurised cylinders and supplied to households through authorised distributors. It provides a convenient cooking option compared with traditional solid fuels.

LPG is considered a cleaner cooking fuel than burning firewood, coal or crop residues inside poorly ventilated homes.

5. What is Ujjwala 2.0?

Ujjwala 2.0 is the second phase of PMUY, launched in August 2021 to provide additional LPG connections to eligible poor households.

  • Additional LPG connections for eligible women.
  • Special facilities for migrant households.
  • Simplified documentation for eligible migrant applicants.
  • Deposit-free LPG connections.
  • Free first refill and stove under the applicable scheme provisions.
Ujjwala 2.0 made it easier for eligible migrant families to apply using a self-declaration instead of certain address and ration-card documents.

6. Main Benefits of PMUY

  • Deposit-free LPG connection for eligible beneficiaries.
  • Government assistance towards the initial connection equipment and installation.
  • Free first LPG refill.
  • Free LPG stove (hotplate) under the applicable connection package.
  • Access to cleaner cooking fuel.
  • Reduced dependence on traditional cooking fuels.
The free first refill and stove are provided as part of the applicable PMUY connection benefits.

7. Financial Assistance for the LPG Connection

The Government of India provides cash assistance towards the initial cost of an eligible PMUY connection. This covers specified connection equipment and installation charges.

  • 14.2 kg cylinder connection: ₹2,050 assistance.
  • 5 kg cylinder connection: ₹1,300 assistance.

The assistance covers items such as:

  • Security deposit for the LPG cylinder.
  • Pressure regulator.
  • Suraksha LPG hose.
  • Domestic Gas Consumer Card.
  • Inspection, installation and demonstration charges.
These figures describe the connection assistance shown on the official PMUY portal. They are not cash payments made directly to the beneficiary.

8. Eligibility Criteria

An applicant must meet the following conditions to receive a PMUY connection:

  • The applicant must be a woman aged 18 years or above.
  • She must belong to a poor household based on the prescribed deprivation declaration.
  • There must not be another LPG connection from any Oil Marketing Company in the same household.
  • The applicant must provide the required KYC and supporting documents.
The current PMUY eligibility information uses the poor-household deprivation declaration. Always check the official application guidelines before submitting an application.

9. Documents Required

Applicants generally need to provide the following documents and information:

  • KYC application form.
  • Aadhaar card of the applicant.
  • Ration card or another accepted document showing family composition.
  • Aadhaar details of adult family members listed in the family document.
  • Proof of address, if the address differs from the Aadhaar address.
  • Bank account details and IFSC code.
  • Prescribed deprivation declaration.
Migrant applicants may use the prescribed self-declaration for certain address and family-document requirements under Ujjwala 2.0.

10. How to Apply for PMUY

Eligible applicants can apply online or visit an authorised LPG distributor.

Online Application:

  • Visit the official PMUY website.
  • Select the option to apply for a new Ujjwala connection.
  • Choose an LPG provider: Indane, Bharatgas or HP Gas.
  • Fill in the required applicant and household details.
  • Upload the necessary documents and submit the application.
  • Complete verification through the authorised distributor.

Offline Application:

  • Visit a nearby authorised LPG distributor.
  • Request the PMUY application and KYC forms.
  • Complete the forms and attach the required documents.
  • Submit the application for verification.
  • After approval, the connection is released according to the scheme process.
Apply only through the official PMUY portal or an authorised LPG distributor. Do not pay unofficial agents promising guaranteed approval.

11. LPG Refill Subsidy

Apart from the initial deposit-free connection, the Government has also provided targeted financial assistance to help PMUY beneficiaries afford LPG refills.

  • Targeted subsidy introduced in May 2022.
  • The subsidy was increased to ₹300 per 14.2 kg cylinder.
  • For 5 kg cylinders, the amount is proportionately adjusted.
  • The subsidy is transferred through the applicable Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) process.
Financial Year Update: The official approval located for FY 2025–26 provided ₹300 per 14.2 kg cylinder for up to 9 refills per year. Do not assume the same subsidy limit automatically applies to FY 2026–27. Check the latest government notification.

12. How Does the Subsidy Reach Beneficiaries?

The refill subsidy is provided through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system under the applicable government guidelines.

  • The beneficiary books an eligible LPG refill.
  • The LPG distributor supplies the cylinder.
  • The transaction is recorded by the Oil Marketing Company.
  • Eligible subsidy is transferred to the beneficiary's linked bank account under the applicable rules.
Beneficiaries should keep their bank details and LPG consumer information updated.

13. Role of Government Departments

  • Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas: Formulates policies and oversees the implementation of PMUY.
  • Oil Marketing Companies: Indian Oil Corporation, Bharat Petroleum and Hindustan Petroleum provide LPG connections through their distributor networks.
  • LPG Distributors: Receive applications, verify documents and provide connections to approved beneficiaries.
  • Government of India: Provides financial assistance and targeted subsidy support under approved guidelines.

14. Benefits for Women and Families

  • Reduces exposure to smoke from traditional cooking fuels.
  • Can improve indoor air quality when LPG replaces solid fuels.
  • Reduces the time spent collecting firewood.
  • Makes everyday cooking more convenient.
  • Supports women's health and well-being.
  • Can help reduce pressure on local forests and biomass resources.

15. Environmental Benefits

  • Encourages a shift away from traditional solid cooking fuels.
  • Can reduce household smoke and particulate pollution.
  • May reduce the use of firewood for cooking.
  • Supports cleaner household energy practices.
LPG is a cleaner cooking fuel than traditional solid fuels, although it is still a fossil fuel. It is not a zero-emission energy source.

16. LPG Safety Guidelines

  • Keep the LPG cylinder upright in a well-ventilated area.
  • Keep the cylinder away from direct heat and flames.
  • Use only approved LPG regulators and hoses.
  • Turn off the regulator when the stove is not in use.
  • Keep combustible materials away from the cooking area.
  • Get the LPG installation inspected by authorised personnel.
  • Follow the safety instructions supplied by the LPG distributor.
If you suspect an LPG leak, avoid operating electrical switches or creating flames. Turn off the regulator if it is safe to do so, ventilate the area and contact the LPG emergency service at 1906.

17. Achievements of PMUY

PMUY has significantly expanded LPG access among economically vulnerable households.

  • The original target of 8 crore connections was achieved in 2019.
  • Ujjwala 2.0 expanded the programme further.
  • More than 10.57 crore PMUY connections had been provided as of May 2026, according to a government publication.
  • The scheme has helped increase access to cleaner cooking fuel across India.
Connection totals are different from the number of refills used. Continued LPG use also depends on household affordability and refill access.

18. Challenges of the Scheme

  • Some households may find regular refill costs difficult to afford.
  • Remote areas can have limited access to LPG distributors.
  • Some families may continue using traditional fuels alongside LPG.
  • Awareness of safe LPG use is important.
  • Regular refill affordability is necessary for sustained adoption.

19. Current Status and Important Updates

PMUY continues to be listed on the official government portal. The portal reported more than 10.56 crore connections released as of 23 September 2026.

The Government approved an additional 25 lakh connections during FY 2025–26 to clear pending applications and expand LPG access.

Important: Connection availability, application processing and refill subsidy limits can change through government notifications. Check the official PMUY portal for the latest information before applying.

20. Important Points to Remember

  • PMUY stands for Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana.
  • It was launched on 1 May 2016.
  • The nodal ministry is the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas.
  • It provides deposit-free LPG connections to eligible adult women from poor households.
  • Ujjwala 2.0 was launched in August 2021.
  • The first refill and stove are provided free under the applicable connection package.
  • Eligible beneficiaries may receive targeted refill subsidy under current government approvals.
  • Applications can be submitted online or through authorised LPG distributors.
  • There should not be another LPG connection in the same household.

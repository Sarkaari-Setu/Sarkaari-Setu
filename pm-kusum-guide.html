PM-KUSUM Yojana

Complete Guide to Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan

1. What is PM-KUSUM Yojana?

PM-KUSUM stands for Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan.

It is a Government of India initiative launched in March 2019 under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE).

The scheme aims to promote solar energy in the agricultural sector by helping farmers install solar power plants and solar agricultural pumps.

2. Main Objectives

  • Provide energy security to farmers.
  • Promote solar-powered agricultural irrigation.
  • Reduce dependence on diesel-powered pumps.
  • Increase farmers' income through solar electricity.
  • Promote renewable and environment-friendly energy.
  • Reduce pollution caused by conventional energy sources.

3. Three Main Components of PM-KUSUM

The scheme operates through three major components:


Component A - Solar Power Plants

Under Component A, farmers and eligible farmer organisations can establish decentralised grid-connected solar power plants on their land.

  • Solar plants can have a capacity of up to 2 MW.
  • Farmers can use suitable barren or agricultural land.
  • Electricity generated is supplied to DISCOMs.
  • Farmers can earn income through the sale of electricity.

Component B - Standalone Solar Pumps

This component supports the installation of standalone solar-powered agricultural pumps, particularly in areas without grid-connected agricultural electricity supply.

  • Helps farmers operate irrigation pumps using solar energy.
  • Reduces dependence on diesel and conventional electricity.
  • Supports irrigation in off-grid agricultural areas.

Component C - Solarisation of Existing Pumps

Component C focuses on solarising existing grid-connected agricultural pumps.

  • Existing agricultural pumps can be solarised.
  • Farmers can generate electricity through solar panels.
  • Includes individual pump solarisation.
  • Also includes feeder-level solarisation.

4. Who Can Apply?

Depending on the component and state guidelines, eligible participants may include:

  • Individual farmers.
  • Groups of farmers.
  • Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs).
  • Cooperative societies.
  • Water User Associations (WUAs).
  • Panchayats and eligible community organisations.
TIP: Eligibility and selection requirements differ between Components A, B and C. Check your state's implementing agency for exact conditions.

5. Government Subsidy Structure

Financial assistance depends on the component, location, benchmark cost and applicable state support.

  • Component A: No direct Central Financial Assistance is provided to the power plant developer under this component.
  • Component B: Central Financial Assistance generally covers 30% of the applicable benchmark cost or tender price, whichever is lower.
  • Component C: Individual pump solarisation generally receives similar central assistance of 30%.
  • Special Regions: Central assistance can be 50% in specified North-Eastern, hilly and island regions.
  • Feeder-Level Solarisation: Central assistance is generally ₹1.05 crore per MW, with different provisions for specified special regions.
IMPORTANT: Under Components B and C, state financial support may also apply. The farmer contributes the remaining amount according to the applicable implementation structure. Exact assistance must be checked with the state agency.

6. Benefits of PM-KUSUM

  • Lower irrigation energy expenses.
  • Reduced diesel consumption.
  • Access to solar-powered irrigation.
  • Additional income opportunities for farmers.
  • Promotion of clean and renewable energy.
  • Reduced environmental pollution.
  • Better energy security for agricultural activities.

7. Documents Generally Required

  • Aadhaar or other accepted identity proof.
  • Land ownership or cultivation-related documents, as applicable.
  • Bank account details.
  • Passport-size photograph.
  • Mobile number.
  • Existing electricity connection details, where applicable.
  • Other documents required by the state implementing agency.

8. How to Apply?

Applications are handled through the designated state implementing agencies. The exact procedure depends on the component and state.

  • Step 1: Visit the official PM-KUSUM portal.
  • Step 2: Check the guidelines and implementing agency for your state.
  • Step 3: Identify the component suitable for your agricultural requirements.
  • Step 4: Check whether applications are currently being accepted in your state.
  • Step 5: Complete the application through the authorised state agency or designated portal.
  • Step 6: Submit the required documents and information.
  • Step 7: Complete verification and other applicable approval procedures.
  • Step 8: Follow the instructions regarding vendor selection, installation and commissioning.

9. Important Things to Remember

  • Check the latest state-specific scheme guidelines.
  • Verify your eligibility before applying.
  • Use only authorised vendors and agencies.
  • Keep your application reference number safe.
  • Check the approved equipment specifications.
  • Understand your contribution before making payments.
  • Keep copies of submitted documents.

10. Beware of Fraudulent Websites

Beware of fake websites, fraudulent agents and messages claiming guaranteed PM-KUSUM registration or subsidies.

Do not share personal or banking information with unauthorised websites.

Always verify application procedures and payment requirements with the official government portal or state implementing agency.

11. Current Application Status

Scheme implementation and application windows are managed through state authorities. The original scheme timeline was extended up to March 2026, with subsequent directions governing the completion of eligible projects.

Therefore, do not assume that fresh applications are currently open. Check the latest MNRE notifications and your state's implementing agency before applying.

📋 Pre-Registration Document Preparation

Keep these exact files ready on your desktop before starting the registration process to prevent session timeouts:

  • Passport Photograph: Recent front-facing color photo on a plain white background (JPG/JPEG format, file size: 20 KB to 200 KB).
  • Signature: Clear scanned signature in dark blue or black ink on white paper (JPG/JPEG format, file size: 10 KB to 100 KB).
  • Category Certificate: SC/ST/OBC (NCL)/EWS certificate issued by a competent authority in PDF format (Up to 1.5 MB).
  • Special Reservation Documents: Domicile Certificate, PwD/SAP Certificate, or BPL Certificate in PDF format (Up to 1.5 MB).
  • Active Credentials: A personal 10-digit mobile number and primary email ID for OTP verification and future correspondence.

Step 2 — Account Registration & OTP Verification

Follow these exact clicking steps to create your applicant account:

  • On the Consortium portal homepage, click on the active section labeled CLAT 2027.
  • Locate the Login/Registration box on the right side of the page and click the blue "Register" button.
  • Type your Full Name exactly as it is printed on your 10th Class Marksheet.
  • Enter your valid 10-digit Mobile Number and primary Email Address.
  • Create a strong password (minimum 6 characters including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols) and re-enter it to confirm.
  • Click "Register". A 5-digit OTP will be sent to your mobile number via SMS.
  • Type the 5-digit OTP into the pop-up box and click "Activate Account".

Step 3 — Log In & Fill Detailed Application Form

Log in using your registered Mobile Number and Password, then complete every tab of the form:

  • Program Selection: Choose UG (5-Year Integrated LL.B.) or PG (1-Year LL.M.) and click "Apply for CLAT".
  • Personal Details Tab: Click "Browse" to upload your saved Passport Photo and Signature. Enter your Gender, Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY), Father's Name, Mother's Name, Nationality, Category, and State Domicile.
  • Communication Details Tab: Enter your full permanent street address, city, state, and 6-digit Pincode. Check the box if your correspondence address is identical.
  • Academic Qualifications Tab: Select your 12th standard status (Appearing or Passed), Board name, and percentage/CGPA obtained.
  • Test City Preferences Tab: Select 3 preferred exam cities in order of priority from the drop-down menus (e.g., Choice 1: Delhi, Choice 2: Noida, Choice 3: Gurgaon).

Step 4 — Document Upload & Online Fee Payment

Finalize your submission through these steps:

  • Upload PDF copies of your Category, Domicile, or PwD certificates under the Documents Upload tab (if applicable).
  • Click the "Preview Application Form" button to carefully double-check all entered information.
  • Check the legal declaration box agreeing to the terms and click "Make Payment".
  • Select your preferred payment gateway method: Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI.
  • Once redirected back to your dashboard with a "Payment Successful" status, click to download and save your final CLAT Application Summary PDF and payment receipt.

📚 CLAT Exam Pattern & Section-wise Syllabus

Complete breakdown of subjects, weightage, marking scheme, and question format for UG & PG law programs.

📋 Exam Structure & Marking Scheme

  • Exam Mode: Offline Pen-and-Paper Test (OMR response sheet using black/blue ballpoint pen).
  • Total Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes). Additional 40 minutes provided for eligible PwD candidates (Total: 2 Hours 40 Minutes).
  • Total Questions: 120 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Marking Scheme: +1.0 Mark for every correct answer; -0.25 Marks deduction for every wrong answer; 0 marks for unattempted questions.
  • Format: Passage-based comprehension (~450 words per passage followed by 4–6 MCQs).

📖 CLAT UG Sectional Weightage & Syllabus

  • 1. Current Affairs including General Knowledge (28–32 Questions / ~25% Weightage): Covers contemporary national and international developments, legal news, constitutional updates, treaties, historical events, art & culture.
  • 2. Legal Reasoning (28–32 Questions / ~25% Weightage): Requires reading legal passages and applying legal rules/principles directly to factual scenarios across Law of Torts, Contracts, Criminal Law, Constitutional Law, and contemporary policies.
  • 3. English Language (22–26 Questions / ~20% Weightage): Tests reading comprehension, main idea extraction, context vocabulary, sentence structure, central arguments, and inferences.
  • 4. Logical Reasoning (22–26 Questions / ~20% Weightage): Passage-based critical reasoning evaluating premises, arguments, assumptions, conclusions, cause-and-effect relationships, and analogies.
  • 5. Quantitative Techniques (10–14 Questions / ~10% Weightage): Passage-based Data Interpretation (DI) using 10th-standard mathematics: Percentages, Ratios & Proportions, Profit & Loss, Mensuration, and Basic Algebra.

⚖️ CLAT PG (LL.M.) Syllabus Breakdown

  • Question Format: 120 Objective MCQs based on legal passages from leading court judgments, statutes, and legal developments.
  • Core Subjects: Constitutional Law, Jurisprudence, Administrative Law, Law of Contract, Torts, Family Law, Criminal Law, Property Law, Company Law, Public International Law, Tax Law, Environmental Law, and Labour & Industrial Law.

🪪 Detailed CLAT e-Admit Card Download Guide

Step-by-step walkthrough to unlock, download, and print your hall ticket.

Step 1 — Lock NLU Preferences to Unlock Admit Card

The Consortium strictly locks hall ticket generation until candidates record their choice of NLUs:

  • Go to the Consortium Portal and log in using your registered Mobile Number and Password.
  • On your dashboard, locate and click the button labeled "Update NLU Preferences".
  • Drag, drop, or enter numbers to rank all National Law Universities (NLUs) in your exact order of choice (1 to 24+).
  • Click "Save Preferences". A confirmation screen will appear confirming your choices are saved.

Step 2 — Download & Print Admit Card (Mid-to-Late November 2026)

Follow these steps once preferences are locked and admit cards are released:

  • Return to your candidate dashboard and click the newly unlocked "Download Admit Card" button.
  • Open the downloaded PDF file and carefully inspect all details: Candidate Name, Roll Number, Exam Center Name & Address, Reporting Time (1:00 PM), and Gate Closure Time (1:30 PM).
  • Print at least two clear color copies of the Admit Card on standard white A4 sheets.

📍 Exam Center Allotment & Exam Day Guidelines

Center allocation rules, exam city updates, allowed items, and venue guidelines.

📋 Center Allotment Policy & City Updates

  • Allocation Method: Exam cities are allocated based on candidate preferences indicated during registration, subject to local venue seat capacity.
  • Center Updates Notice (CLAT 2027): Vellore and Tiruvarur were officially removed as test cities for CLAT 2027. Affected candidates who selected either location must update their exam city choices on the portal prior to October 31, 2026.

🧰 Permissible Items inside Exam Room (Sunday, December 6, 2026)

Carry only the following allowed items inside your transparent exam folder:

  • Printed copy of your CLAT 2027 e-Admit Card.
  • Original valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License).
  • At least two Black or Blue Ballpoint Pens with transparent barrels (Gel pens are strictly prohibited on OMR sheets).
  • Transparent water bottle (up to 1 Litre).
  • Simple analog wristwatch (Smartwatches, calculators, and digital devices are strictly banned).

🏆 Scorecard, Merit List & NLU Counselling Guide

Checking your scorecard, understanding ranks, and navigating the centralized admission process.

📋 Checking Scorecard & Ranks (3rd Week of December 2026)

  • Log in to the Consortium portal using your mobile number and password.
  • Click on the "View Scorecard" button on your dashboard.
  • Review your Total Score, Section-wise Breakdown, All India Rank (AIR), Category Rank, and State Domicile Rank.
  • Download and save a copy of your Scorecard PDF for document verification during admissions.

Step 1 — Centralized Counselling & Seat Allocation (December 2026 / January 2027)

Participate in the multi-round NLU counselling process:

  • Counselling Invite: Shortlisted candidates receive an invite banner on their dashboard along with an SMS alert.
  • Registration Fee: Click "Register for Counselling" and pay the non-refundable fee online (₹30,000 for General; ₹20,000 for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD).
  • Seat Options upon Allotment: Select "Freeze" (lock allotted seat), "Float" (accept provisionally with upgrade option in next round), or "Exit" (withdraw from process).

❓ Common Issues, Discrepancies & Helpdesk

Solutions for payment pending errors, admit card locks, and official contact support.

📋 Troubleshooting & Official Contacts

  • Payment Deducted but Status Pending: Wait up to 24 hours for bank server reconciliation. If duplicate payments occur, excess amounts are automatically refunded to the source account after registration closes.
  • Admit Card Download Locked: Fill out and save your NLU preference list to unlock the download button on your dashboard.
  • Admit Card / Scorecard Discrepancy: Send an immediate email to the Consortium Helpdesk with your Application Number and a clear scan of your 10th Class Marksheet attached.

🛡️ UPPSC UP PCS Application & One Time Registration (OTR) Guide

Comprehensive click-by-click guide for completing your UP PCS OTR profiling and application on the official UPPSC portal.

📋 Pre-Registration Requirements & Eligibility Overview

Gather these details and verify your educational and age eligibility before opening the portal:

  • Active Contact Info: Mobile Number and Personal Email ID (linked with Aadhaar for OTP verification).
  • Government Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Driving License. Keep scanned copies ready.
  • Educational Qualification: Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline from a recognized university by the closing date of application. (Age limit: 21 to 40 years, with upper-age relaxations for UP reserved categories).
  • Passport Photograph & Signature: Scanned photo and signature in JPG/JPEG format conforming to UPPSC size and dimension guidelines (Photo: 3.5cm x 4.5cm, Signature: 3.5cm x 1.5cm).
  • Category Certificate Details: Caste/Category certificate number, issuance authority, and date (if claiming reservation as UP Domicile).

Step 1 — Navigate to the Official UPPSC OTR Portal

Open your web browser and access the official Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission One Time Registration portal.

Open UPPSC OTR Portal →

Step 2 — Complete One Time Registration (OTR) Profile

If you are applying to UPPSC for the first time, OTR is mandatory before filling any exam application form:

  • Click on the "Registration / Sign Up" button on the OTR homepage.
  • Authenticate using your Aadhaar Number or email/mobile OTP verification.
  • Enter your basic personal details exactly as recorded in your High School (10th) marksheet:
    • Candidate's Full Name, Gender, and Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY)
    • Father's Name & Mother's Name
    • Domicile State and Category (General / EWS / OBC / SC / ST)
    • High School Board Name, Passing Year, and Roll Number
  • Review all entered details carefully, generate login credentials (Password), and submit.
  • Your permanent OTR Number will be generated instantly and sent to your registered mobile and email. Save this number securely.

Step 3 — Log In & Access UP PCS Examination Application

Once your OTR profile is locked and verified, proceed with the PCS application:

  • Go to the main UPPSC recruitment portal at uppsc.up.nic.in.
  • Click on "All Notifications / Advertisements (יबविज्ञापन / विज्ञप्तियां)".
  • Locate "Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services Examination (UP PCS)".
  • Click on "Apply (आवेदन करें)" and log in using your OTR Number. Fetch your OTR details automatically into the application form.

Step 4 — Fill Form Details, Pay Fee & Finalize Submission

Complete the remaining sections of the application:

  • Post Preferences & Qualifications: Select your preferred executive/technical posts based on your graduation degree and specific eligibility criteria (e.g., Sub Registrar, District Audit Officer, etc.).
  • Exam Fee Payment: Pay the required examination fee online via Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI.
    • Unreserved / OBC / EWS: ₹125 (₹100 exam fee + ₹25 online processing fee)
    • SC / ST / Ex-Servicemen: ₹65
    • Disabled (Divyang): ₹25
  • Photograph & Signature Verification: Verify that your photo and signature pulled from your OTR profile are clear and correctly aligned.
  • Final Submission: Click "Submit Application". Download and save the final Application Confirmation PDF for future verification.

📍 Exam Center Allotment & District Preferences

How center allocation functions across UP districts and how to manage center preferences.

📋 District Allocation Policy

UPPSC conducts the PCS Prelims and Mains examinations across multiple designated districts in Uttar Pradesh:

  • District Choice Selection: During Part-II of the application form, candidates are prompted to select their preferred exam districts (typically up to 3 to 5 choices depending on notification rules).
  • Administrative Allotment: The commission assigns exam centers based on candidate preference, seat capacity, and administrative feasibility. Female candidates and PwD candidates are generally given priority consideration for home districts or nearby regions.
  • Non-Changeable Rule: Once the exam center/district is locked on your admit card, requests for center changes are strictly not entertained by UPPSC under any circumstances.

⚠️ Addressing Center Discrepancies

If your admit card displays an error in exam venue address or district allocation:

  • Verify Confirmation Page: Cross-check the district choices listed on your final submitted application PDF.
  • Contact Control Room: Immediately reach out to the UPPSC helpline or visit the Prayagraj commission office counter with your application printout.
  • Lodge Online Grievance: Submit a formal ticket via the online grievance redressal module on the official UPPSC website.

🪪 Detailed UP PCS e-Admit Card Download Guide

Step-by-step instructions to download, verify, and prepare your hall ticket for Prelims and Mains.

📋 Download Requirements

Keep these credentials ready when UPPSC releases the e-Admit Card (usually 10–15 days prior to the exam date):

  • UPPSC OTR Number or Registration ID.
  • Candidate's Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) and Gender.
  • Captcha verification code displayed on screen.

Step 1 — Access the Admit Card Portal

Click the link below to open the official UPPSC admit card download gateway.

Open UPPSC Admit Card Portal →

Step 2 — Input Details & Download PDF

Follow these quick steps on the portal page:

  • Enter your OTR Number / Registration ID.
  • Select your Date of Birth and Gender from the dropdown menus.
  • Enter the verification Verification Code (Captcha) exactly as shown.
  • Click on "Download Admit Card". Print 2 clear copies of the hall ticket.

Step 3 — Exam Day Timings & Shift Schedule

Verify your paper schedules and exam timings printed on the admit card:

  • Paper I (General Studies - I): 09:30 AM – 11:30 AM (Reporting time: 08:00 AM)
  • Paper II (General Studies - II / CSAT): 02:30 PM – 04:30 PM (Reporting time: 01:00 PM)
  • Mandatory Kit: Printed e-Admit Card, two passport-size photographs, and a valid original Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.).

🗓️ Official Answer Key & Objection Cycle

UPPSC follows a structured protocol for publishing official keys:

  • Provisional Answer Key: Released on the official website within 3 to 5 days following the Prelims examination across Series (A, B, C, D).
  • Objection Window: Candidates are given a 5-day window to submit online objections against disputed questions along with standard standard-textbook proof and a prescribed fee per question.
  • Final Answer Key: Published along with or just before the declaration of the Prelims written results after expert panel review.

🏆 Prelims Results, Mains Application & Interview Guide

Navigating the multi-stage selection process: Prelims qualification, Mains detailed application form (DAF), and Viva-Voce.

📋 Multi-Stage Examination Structure

The UP PCS selection process consists of three rigorous successive stages:

  • Stage 1 — Preliminary Examination: Objective type (GS-1 and CSAT qualifying). Shortlists candidates roughly 13-15 times the number of vacancies for Mains.
  • Stage 2 — Main Examination (Written): Descriptive style consisting of General Hindi (150 marks), Essay (150 marks), and 6 General Studies papers (GS 1 to GS 6, 200 marks each). Total written = 1500 marks. (Note: Optional subjects have been removed from the UP PCS syllabus).
  • Stage 3 — Personality Test (Viva-Voce): 100 marks evaluating administrative aptitude, clarity of expression, and general awareness.

Step 1 — Check Prelims Result & Fill Mains Application

Once the Prelims result PDF is published:

  • Visit the "Results" section on uppsc.up.nic.in and search your roll number in the qualified candidate merit list PDF.
  • Qualified candidates must immediately fill out the Online Application for Main Examination (Form-B) and pay the Mains exam fee.
  • Take a printout of the filled Mains application form and dispatch hard copies along with self-attested copies of all educational and category certificates to the UPPSC Prayagraj office within the stipulated deadline.

❓ Common Issues, Technical Errors & UPPSC Helpdesk

Troubleshooting OTR lock errors, fee reconciliation failures, and official contact channels.

📋 Resolving Technical & Application Errors

  • OTR Modification Lock: If you made an error in your OTR profile before submitting an exam form, use the OTR edit/correction window provided periodically by UPPSC. Basic details like name/dob locked after final verification require offline representation.
  • Fee Payment Deduction Failure: If money is debited from your account but the payment status shows pending/unconfirmed, wait 48 hours for automatic bank reconciliation or use the "Verify Payment Status" link on the portal before paying a second time.
  • Official UPPSC Helpdesk Contact Channels:
    • Helpline Phone Numbers: 0532-2407585, 0532-2407586
    • Official Email Support: online.uppsc@nic.in
    • Working Hours: Monday to Friday (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM)

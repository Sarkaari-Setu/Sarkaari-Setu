🛡️ UPSC CDS Application & Registration Guide

Comprehensive click-by-click guide for completing your Combined Defence Services application on the UPSC portal.

📋 Pre-Registration Requirements & Eligibility Overview

Gather these details and verify your academy-specific educational eligibility before opening the application portal:

  • Active Contact Info: Mobile Number and Personal Email ID (must remain active for at least 1 year).
  • Government Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, PAN Card, Driving License, or Photo ID. Keep a scanned PDF copy (20 KB to 300 KB).
  • Educational Qualification (Degree Specifics):
    • IMA & OTA: Graduation Degree in any discipline from a recognized university.
    • INA: Bachelor of Engineering / B.Tech Degree.
    • AFA: Bachelor's Degree with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 level OR a Bachelor of Engineering.
  • Passport Photograph: Color photo in JPG/JPEG format (20 KB to 300 KB, minimum resolution 350x350 pixels).
  • Scanned Signature: Signature in black ink on white paper in JPG/JPEG format (20 KB to 300 KB, minimum resolution 350x350 pixels).
  • Academy Preferences: Priority order (1 to 4) for IMA, INA, AFA, and OTA (Men/Women) based on your age and educational eligibility.

Step 1 — Navigate to the Official UPSC Portal

Open your web browser (Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox recommended) and access the official registration portal by clicking below.

Open UPSC Online Portal →

Step 2 — Complete One-Time Registration (OTR)

Follow these precise steps if you are applying on the UPSC portal for the first time:

  • Click on the button labeled "OTR (ONE TIME REGISTRATION) FOR UPSC EXAMINATIONS" on the homepage.
  • Click the "New Registration" tab at the top right of the screen.
  • Enter your basic personal details exactly as printed on your 10th Class / Graduation Certificate:
    • Full Name (Do not use prefixes like Mr., Ms., etc.)
    • Gender & Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY)
    • Father's Name & Mother's Name
    • Minority Status, Mobile Number, and Email ID
    • 10th Board Examination Roll Number
  • Set two Security Questions and answers for account recovery.
  • Type the Captcha code shown on the screen and click "Submit".
  • Verify the separate OTPs sent to both your registered Mobile Number and Email ID.
  • Once verified, your permanent OTR ID will be generated and sent via SMS and Email. Save this for all future attempts.

Step 3 — Log In & Access Part-I Application

Once your OTR profile is ready, proceed with the main CDS application form:

  • Return to the login screen at upsconline.nic.in.
  • Select your login method: Email ID, Mobile Number, or OTR ID.
  • Click "Get OTP", type the received OTP, enter the Captcha, and click "Login".
  • On your Candidate Dashboard, click on the "Latest Notification" tab.
  • Locate "Combined Defence Services Examination" and click the blue button marked "Click Here for Part-I (भाग-I के लिए यहां क्लिक करें)".
  • Read all rules carefully, scroll to the bottom, and click "Yes" to accept the declaration.

Step 4 — Fill Part-I Form & Academy Preferences

Complete the detailed fields under the Part-I form:

  • Personal Details: Verify pre-filled OTR data (Community/Category, Fee Exemption eligibility, Marital Status).
  • Educational Qualification Code: Select your current qualification status:
    • Passed/Appearing Graduation (General/Arts/Commerce/Science): Eligible for IMA and OTA.
    • Passed/Appearing Engineering Degree: Eligible for INA, IMA, AFA, and OTA.
    • Passed Graduation with Physics & Maths at 10+2 Level: Eligible for AFA, IMA, and OTA.
  • Academy Preferences: Enter numbers from 1 to 4 in order of preference for IMA, INA, AFA, and OTA. Enter 0 for any academy you are not eligible for or do not wish to opt for.
  • NCC 'C' Certificate Details: Mention if you hold an NCC 'C' Certificate (Army/Navy/Air Force Wing) to claim vacancy reservation/preference.
  • Click "Continue" to review your details, enter the Captcha, and click "I Agree". Your Registration ID (RID) will be displayed on screen. Print or screenshot this page.

Step 5 — Fee Payment & Part-II (Document Upload)

Complete the final submission phase:

  • Fee Payment (if applicable): Pay the ₹200 application fee online via Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI. (All Female candidates, SC, and ST candidates are fully exempted from fee payment).
  • Upload Photo & Signature: Upload your scanned JPEG files for Photograph and Signature. Click "Upload Image" and verify the preview.
  • Upload Photo ID PDF: Upload the scanned PDF copy of the Photo ID card whose number you entered in Part-I.
  • Select Exam Center: Choose your preferred examination city from the dropdown menu.
  • Final Submission: Click "I have read Declaration Official and Agree" and submit. Download and save your final Application Confirmation PDF.

📍 Exam Center Allotment & Change Requests

How center allocation functions, how to verify venue details, and emergency procedures.

📋 How Center Allocation Works

UPSC enforces a strict automated allocation policy for all examination centers across India:

  • "First-Apply-First-Allot" Principle: Each exam city has a fixed maximum candidate capacity. Once a city reaches its limit, that option is automatically frozen on the portal.
  • Early Submission Benefit: Candidates who apply within the first few days of notification release secure their preferred 1st choice center city.
  • No Manual Overrides: Center allocation is locked in the main database upon final submission and cannot be manually modified through routine edit options.

⚠️ Handling Wrong Center Allotment or Venue Errors

If your admit card displays an incorrect venue or unexpected city, execute the following steps immediately:

  • Check Application Slip: Open your saved Part-II application PDF and double-check the exam city you selected during submission.
  • Report System Discrepancies: If the city on your admit card differs from your submitted confirmation slip, draft an email immediately to uscds-upsc@nic.in with the subject line: "URGENT: Center Discrepancy - CDS Examination - [Your Roll No/RID]".
  • Attach Proof: Attach a copy of your Part-II Application Confirmation Page and your downloaded e-Admit Card.
  • Lodge Formal Grievance: Visit the official UPSC e-Grievance portal (e-SUMMIT) to log an official grievance ticket.

🪪 Detailed CDS e-Admit Card Download Guide

Step-by-step instructions to download, verify, and prepare your hall ticket for exam day.

📋 Download Requirements

Keep these exact credentials ready when UPSC releases the admit card (typically 2 to 3 weeks before the exam date):

  • UPSC Registration ID (RID) OR Assigned Roll Number.
  • Date of Birth as registered during OTR profiling.
  • Access to a printer connected to a PC/laptop.

Step 1 — Access the Download Portal

Click the link below to open the dedicated e-Admit Card portal in a new tab.

Open UPSC e-Admit Card Portal →

Step 2 — Portal Navigation & Identity Choice

Follow these steps on the UPSC e-Admit Card page:

  • Locate "Combined Defence Services Examination" in the active notifications list.
  • Click on the "Click Here / यहाँ क्लिक करें" link under the download column.
  • Read the instructions page thoroughly, scroll down, and click "Yes" to confirm reading.
  • Select your identification choice:
    • By Registration ID (RID): Enter your 11-digit RID number and Date of Birth.
    • By Roll Number: Enter your 7-digit Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  • Type the Captcha image letters into the box and click "Submit".

Step 3 — Verification & Physical Printing

Once the PDF opens on your screen, execute these essential steps:

  • Verify All Printed Fields: Check your Full Name, Roll Number, Exam Center Name, Venue Address, and Subject Timings:
    • English: 09:00 AM – 11:00 AM
    • General Knowledge: 12:00 PM – 02:00 PM
    • Elementary Mathematics (IMA/INA/AFA only): 03:00 PM – 05:00 PM
  • Print Color Copies: Print at least 2 clear color copies of the e-Admit Card on standard white A4 sheets.
  • Prepare Exam Day Kit: Staple the e-Admit Card together with the original Photo ID card (whose number is printed on the admit card) and two recent passport-size photographs.

🏆 Written Results, Merit List & SSB Interview Guide

How to check your written result, access roll lists, and complete mandatory SSB registration.

📋 Types of Result Publications

UPSC issues written results in two sequential stages on their portal:

  • Stage 1 — Roll Number List PDF: Released within 35–45 days of the written exam. Contains the Roll Numbers of candidates qualified for SSB Interviews.
  • Stage 2 — Name-wise List PDF: Released 3–5 days after the roll number list. Displays candidates' full names alongside their Roll Numbers and shortlisted academies.

Step 1 — Check Written Examination Result

Follow these steps to check if you have qualified for the SSB Interview:

  • Click the link below to go to the official UPSC Written Results section.
  • Locate "Written Result: Combined Defence Services Examination" and click the download PDF link.
  • Open the PDF on your device and press Ctrl + F (or tap the search icon on mobile).
  • Type your 7-digit Roll Number and press Enter. If highlighted, you have cleared the written stage.
Go to UPSC Written Results Board →

Step 2 — Mandatory Registration for SSB Interview

Candidates who qualify for IMA, INA, AFA, or OTA must register on the respective service portal within 14 days of result declaration:

  • Army / IMA / OTA Candidates: Visit joinindianarmy.nic.in, click on "Officer Entry Login", and complete registration to receive SSB call-up letters.
  • Navy / INA Candidates: Visit this and log in to select your SSB center and batch date.
  • Air Force / AFA Candidates: Visit careerindianairforce.cdac.in or afcat.cdac.in to register and choose your AFSB center.

❓ Common Issues, Discrepancies & UPSC Helpdesk

Solutions for technical errors, rejections, blurred credentials, and official contact channels.

📋 Resolving Discrepancies & Common Application Errors

  • Blurred Photo or Signature on Admit Card: If your photo or signature appears blurred on the printed e-Admit Card, bring two passport-size photographs along with a printed self-declaration and valid original photo ID to the exam venue.
  • Application Rejected Due to Fee Non-Receipt: If your application status shows rejected due to payment non-confirmation, present your bank transaction receipt or SBI Pay-in-Slip proof to the UPSC Facilitation Counter.
  • OTR Correction Window: UPSC opens a 7-day correction window right after the application closing date. You can edit fields in your OTR profile once during this period.
  • Contact UPSC Facilitation Counter: Candidates can contact the UPSC facilitation counter near Gate C of their campus in New Delhi in person or via telephone on working days (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM):
    • 011-23385271
    • 011-23381125
    • 011-23098543

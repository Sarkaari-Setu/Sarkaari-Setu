🗳️ Right to Vote in India

Understanding Universal Adult Suffrage and Constitutional Guarantees under Article 326.

📋 Constitutional Foundation — Article 326

In India, the Right to Vote is a fundamental constitutional right governed by Article 326 of the Constitution of India. It establishes Universal Adult Suffrage, guaranteeing that every Indian citizen meeting the basic age and residence criteria has the right to vote without discrimination based on religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth.

📋 Voter Eligibility Criteria

To qualify as an eligible voter for elections in India, a citizen must fulfill the following conditions:

  • Citizenship: Must be a legal Indian citizen.
  • Age Limit: Must be at least 18 years old on the qualifying date (1st January, 1st April, 1st July, or 1st October of the revision year).
  • Ordinary Residence: Must be an ordinary resident of the constituency where registration is sought.
  • Legal Qualifications: Must not be disqualified under any law relating to corrupt practices, criminal offences, or unsoundness of mind.

Step 1 — Verify Your Voting Status

Possessing an Voter ID (EPIC) card is not enough to vote; your name must be actively listed on the official electoral roll of your constituency on the day of election.

🏛️ What Election Is & Election Commission of India (ECI)

An overview of democratic elections in India and the autonomous constitutional body administering them.

📋 What is an Election?

An election is a formal democratic decision-making process by which citizens choose their representatives to hold public office. In India, elections take place at three levels: National (Lok Sabha), State (Legislative Assemblies), and Local Bodies (Panchayats and Municipalities).

🏛️ About the Election Commission of India (ECI)

Established under Article 324 of the Constitution, the Election Commission of India (ECI) is an autonomous, permanent constitutional authority responsible for administering free, fair, and transparent elections across the country.

The ECI consists of the Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) and two Election Commissioners (ECs) operating from Nirvachan Sadan, New Delhi.

📋 Core Powers & Functions of ECI

The Election Commission handles the complete end-to-end management of Indian elections:

  • Electoral Rolls: Preparing, updating, and revising nationwide voter lists.
  • Election Scheduling: Announcing dates and schedules for Parliamentary and State Assembly elections.
  • Model Code of Conduct (MCC): Enforcing ethical guidelines for political parties and candidates during election periods.
  • Party Recognition: Registering political parties and allocating official election symbols.
  • Polling & Counting: Setting up polling stations, deploying Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) / VVPATs, and managing vote counting.

📜 Electoral Roll Registration & Appeals Guide

How to search your name, apply for voter registration, correct details, or file appeals.

Step 1 — Search Name in Electoral Roll

Visit the official ECI voter search engine or Voter Services Portal. Search using your EPIC Number, Mobile Number, or Personal Details to confirm your polling station and serial number.

Step 2 — Select the Appropriate Registration Form

Choose the correct digital form depending on your application requirement:

  • Form 6: Application for new voter registration / shifting from another constituency.
  • Form 6A: Registration form for Overseas (NRI) electors.
  • Form 7: Objection against inclusion or request for deletion of name in electoral roll.
  • Form 8: Correction of entry, shifting of residence, replacement of EPIC card, or marking disability status.

Step 3 — Submit Verification Proofs

Upload digital copies of valid identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card), age proof, and proof of residence (utility bills, rent agreement, bank passbook).

Step 4 — Filing Appeals & Objections

If your application is rejected or an error persists, you can file a formal appeal before the Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) or District Election Officer (DEO) within the prescribed time limit.

📱 ECI Digital Apps & Web Applications Guide

Complete catalog of mobile applications and official web portals developed by the Election Commission of India (ECI).

📲 Category 1: ECI Mobile Applications (ECINET Suite)

Mobile applications designed for citizens, electors, and candidates for on-the-go access via smartphones.

  • Voter Registration Forms: Mobile interface to fill and submit Form 6, 6A, 7, and 8 for registration and corrections.
  • Track your Application Status: Real-time tracking of submitted voter applications using your reference ID.
  • Voter Card Complaints: Register and monitor grievances regarding EPIC card issuance, delivery, or errors.
  • Search your name in Voter List: Search electoral rolls using your EPIC number or personal details to verify polling booth location.
  • Download e-Voter Card (e-EPIC): Download a digitally signed PDF copy of your voter ID directly to your phone.
  • Connect with Election Officials: Access contact details for your local Booth Level Officer (BLO), ERO, and DEO.
  • Divyang (Saksham App): Accessibility features allowing PwD voters to request wheelchairs, pick-up facilities, and voter assistance.
  • Book A Call With BLO: Schedule a direct phone callback or interaction with your designated Booth Level Officer.
  • Know Your Candidates (KYC): Inspect candidate affidavits, criminal antecedents, educational background, and financial assets.
  • Model Code of Conduct Complaint (cVIGIL): Report election code violations or voter bribery with geotagged photo/video proof for 100-minute resolution.
  • Past Election: Access historical election archives, past voting statistics, and detailed candidate reports.
  • Current Election: View live election schedules, constituency phases, and active candidate information.
  • Polling Trends (Voter Turnout): Monitor estimated real-time voter turnout percentages on polling days.
  • Suvidha 2.0 Mobile: Candidate interface to submit campaign requests and track election nomination approvals on mobile devices.

💻 Category 2: ECI Web Applications & Administrative Portals

Web-based portals used by citizens, candidates, political parties, and election officials for comprehensive administrative workflows.

  • Suvidha 2.0 (Candidate Nomination): Web portal for candidates to fill and register official nomination forms online.
  • ENCORE (Candidate Scrutiny): Internal system for Returning Officers to process, accept, reject, or record withdrawal of candidate nominations.
  • ENCORE (Election Permissions): Portal for political parties and candidates to request rally, loudspeaker, vehicle, and helipad campaign permits.
  • Affidavit Portal: Transparency portal displaying public view of candidate sworn affidavits, financial disclosures, and criminal records.
  • Voter Turnout Portal: Web dashboard displaying phase-wise estimated voter turnout statistics during polling hours.
  • ENCORE (Results & Counting): Secure web platform used by Returning Officers to log round-wise vote counts on counting day.
  • Election Trends TV: Live automated results streaming system designed for media broadcasting and public display screens.
  • ENCORE (Index Card & Statistical Reporting): Automated post-election tool generating statistical reports and official index cards.
  • ENCORE (Expenditure Monitoring): Web platform to track and monitor candidate campaign expenditures and public rally expenses.
  • cVIGIL Portal (Backend): Control center portal for election officers and flying squads to assign and resolve cVIGIL citizen reports.
  • EMS 2.0 (EVM Management System): Inventory management portal tracking allocation, movement, and physical location of EVMs and VVPATs.
  • SVEEP Website: Electoral awareness portal offering voter education materials, voter pledges, and outreach programs.
  • RTI Online Portal: Digital portal allowing citizens to submit Right to Information (RTI) applications directly to the ECI.
  • PPRTMS (Political Parties Registration Tracking): Tracking portal allowing political parties to monitor their official registration status.
  • ERO NET: Backend web network used nationwide by Electoral Registration Officers to process electoral roll data.
  • Voter's Services Portal (voters.eci.gov.in): Primary citizen web portal for voter registration, EPIC downloads, and address shifting.
  • ETPBS (Electronically Transmitted Postal Ballot System): Secure platform for transmitting postal ballot papers electronically to service voters.
  • Service Voter Registration Portal: Registration portal tailored for defense personnel and diplomatic staff serving away from home.
  • Observer Portal: Communication network connecting the ECI with deputed General, Police, and Expenditure Observers.
  • National Grievance Services Portal (NGSP): Centralized grievance redressal web portal for filing and tracking voter complaints.
  • Integrated Election Expenditure Monitoring System: Comprehensive portal monitoring political party and candidate campaign finances.

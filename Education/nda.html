🛡️ UPSC NDA Application & Registration Guide

Comprehensive click-by-click walkthrough for completing your application on the UPSC portal.

📋 Pre-Registration Requirements & Documents

Before launching the browser, prepare the following files and information to prevent session timeouts during registration:

  • Active Contact Info: Mobile Number and Personal Email ID (must remain active for at least 1 year).
  • Government Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, PAN Card, Driving License, or School/Photo ID. Keep a scanned PDF copy (20 KB to 300 KB).
  • Educational Certificates: 10th Class Marksheet/Certificate (for exact Date of Birth matching) and 12th Class Marksheet or Admit Card (if appearing).
  • Passport Photograph: Color photo in JPG/JPEG format (20 KB to 300 KB, minimum resolution 350x350 pixels).
  • Scanned Signature: Signature in black ink on white paper in JPG/JPEG format (20 KB to 300 KB, minimum resolution 350x350 pixels).
  • Wing Order of Preference: Decide your priority sequence (1 to 4) for Army, Air Force, Navy, and Naval Academy (10+2 Cadet Entry).

Step 1 — Navigate to the Official UPSC Portal

Open your desktop browser (Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox recommended) and access the official registration page by clicking below.

Open UPSC Online Portal →

Step 2 — Complete One-Time Registration (OTR)

Follow these precise steps if you are registering on the UPSC portal for the first time:

  • Click on the button labeled "OTR (ONE TIME REGISTRATION) FOR UPSC EXAMINATIONS" on the homepage.
  • Click the "New Registration" tab at the top right of the screen.
  • Enter your basic personal details exactly as printed on your 10th Class Certificate:
    • Full Name (Do not use prefixes like Mr., Miss, etc.)
    • Gender & Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY)
    • Father's Name & Mother's Name
    • Minority Status, Mobile Number, and Email ID
    • 10th Board Examination Roll Number
  • Set two Security Questions and answers for password recovery.
  • Type the Captcha code shown on the screen and click "Submit".
  • Verify the OTPs sent to both your registered Mobile Number and Email ID.
  • Once verified, your unique OTR ID will be generated and sent via SMS and Email. Save this carefully.

Step 3 — Log In & Access Part-I Application

Once your OTR profile is created, follow this sequence to fill out the NDA Application Form:

  • Return to the login screen by clicking this.
  • Select your login method: Email ID, Mobile Number, or OTR ID.
  • Click "Get OTP", enter the OTP received, type the Captcha, and click "Login".
  • On your Candidate Dashboard, click on the "Latest Notification" tab.
  • Locate "National Defence Academy & Naval Academy Examination" and click on the blue button marked "Click Here for Part-I (भाग-I के लिए यहां क्लिक करें)".
  • Read all instructions on the screen carefully, scroll to the bottom, and click "Yes" to accept the declaration.

Step 4 — Fill Part-I Form & Service Preferences

Complete the detailed fields under the Part-I form:

  • Personal Details: Verify pre-filled OTR data (Community/Category, Fee Concession eligibility, Marital Status - must be Unmarried).
  • Educational Qualification: Select your current status from the dropdown:
    • Code 1: Passed 12th Class under 10+2 pattern without Physics & Mathematics (Army Wing only).
    • Code 2: Passed 12th Class with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics (All Wings).
    • Code 3: Appearing in 12th Class without Physics & Mathematics.
    • Code 4: Appearing in 12th Class with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics.
  • Service Preferences: Enter numbers from 1 to 4 in order of preference for Army, Navy, Air Force, and Naval Academy. Enter 0 for any wing you do not wish to opt for.
  • Sainik/Military School Details: Specify if you are a student of a Sainik/Military School or Son of NCO/OR/Other Ranks.
  • Click "Continue" to review your details, type the Captcha, and click "I Agree" to lock Part-I. Your Registration ID (RID) will be displayed. Print or screenshot this page.

Step 5 — Payment & Part-II (Document Upload)

Proceed to complete the second stage of the application:

  • Fee Payment (if applicable): Select your payment mode: Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card, or UPI (₹100 fee). Female, SC, ST candidates, and sons of NCOs/ORs skip this step automatically.
  • Upload Photo & Signature: Browse and select your saved JPEG files for Photo and Signature. Click "Upload Image". Ensure both preview clearly on the screen without blur.
  • Upload Photo ID PDF: Select and upload the scanned PDF file of the Photo ID card whose number you entered in Part-I.
  • Center Selection: Choose your preferred examination city from the dropdown menu.
  • Final Declaration: Scroll down, click "I have read Declaration Official and Agree", and submit. Download and save the final Application PDF.

📍 Exam Center Allotment & Change Requests

How center allocation functions, how to verify venue details, and emergency procedures.

📋 How Center Allocation Works

UPSC enforces a strict automated allocation policy for all examination centers across India:

  • "First-Apply-First-Allot" Principle: Each exam city has a fixed maximum candidate ceiling. Once a city reaches its full capacity, that option is automatically frozen on the portal.
  • Early Submission Benefit: Candidates who apply within the first few days of notification release get their preferred 1st choice center. Late applicants may be forced to choose an alternate city.
  • No Manual Overrides: Once an application is submitted, the center choice is locked into the main system database.

⚠️ Handling Wrong Center Allotment or Venue Errors

If your admit card shows a wrong venue or an unexpected center city, take the following actions immediately:

  • Check Application Slip: Open your saved Part-II application PDF and verify the center name you selected during form submission.
  • Report System Discrepancies: If the city on your admit card differs from your submitted confirmation slip, draft an email immediately to usnda-upsc@nic.in with the subject line: "URGENT: Center Discrepancy - NDA Examination - [Your Roll No/RID]".
  • Attach Proof: Attach a copy of your Part-II Application Confirmation Page and your downloaded e-Admit Card.
  • Lodge Formal Grievance: Visit the official UPSC e-Grievance portal (e-SUMMIT) to log an official ticket.

🪪 Detailed NDA e-Admit Card Download Guide

Step-by-step instructions to download, verify, and prepare your hall ticket for exam day.

📋 Download Requirements

Keep these exact credentials ready when UPSC releases the admit card (typically 3 weeks before the exam date):

  • UPSC Registration ID (RID) OR Assigned Roll Number.
  • Date of Birth as registered during OTR profiling.
  • Access to a printer connected to a PC/laptop.

Step 1 — Access the Download Portal

Click the link below to open the dedicated e-Admit Card portal in a new tab.

Open UPSC e-Admit Card Portal →

Step 2 — Portal Navigation & Identity Choice

Follow these steps on the UPSC e-Admit Card page:

  • Locate "National Defence Academy & Naval Academy Examination" in the active list.
  • Click on the "Click Here / यहाँ क्लिक करें" link under the download column.
  • Read the instructions page thoroughly, scroll down, and click "Yes" to confirm reading.
  • Select your preferred identification option:
    • By Registration ID (RID): Enter your 11-digit RID number and Date of Birth.
    • By Roll Number: Enter your 7-digit Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  • Type the random Captcha image letters into the box and click "Submit".

Step 3 — Verification & Physical Printing

Once the PDF opens on your screen, execute these essential steps:

  • Verify All Printed Fields: Check your Full Name, Roll Number, Exam Center Name, Sub-center Address, Time Table (Maths: 10:00 AM - 12:30 PM | GAT: 2:00 PM - 4:30 PM), and Photo/Signature clarity.
  • Print Color Copies: Print at least 2 clear color copies of the e-Admit Card on standard white A4 sheets.
  • Prepare Exam Day Kit: Staple the e-Admit Card together with the original Photo ID card (whose number is printed on the admit card) and two recent passport-size photographs (with name and date printed on the front).

🏆 Written Results, Merit List & SSB Interview Guide

How to check your written status, access roll lists, and complete mandatory SSB registration.

📋 Types of Result Publications

UPSC issues written results in two sequential stages on their portal:

  • Stage 1 — Roll Number List PDF: Released within 35-45 days of the written exam. Contains only the Roll Numbers of qualified candidates.
  • Stage 2 — Name-wise List PDF: Released 3-5 days after the roll number list. Displays candidates' full names alongside their Roll Numbers.

Step 1 — Check Written Examination Result

Follow these steps to check if you have qualified for the SSB Interview:

  • Click the link below to go to the official UPSC Written Results section.
  • Locate "Written Result: National Defence Academy & Naval Academy Examination" and click the download PDF link.
  • Open the PDF on your device and press Ctrl + F (or tap the search icon on mobile).
  • Type your 7-digit Roll Number and press Enter. If highlighted, you have cleared the written stage.
Go to UPSC Written Results Board →

Step 2 — Mandatory Registration for SSB Interview

Clearing the written exam is only half the process. You must register with the Armed Forces within 14 days of result declaration:

  • Army / Navy / Air Force Candidates: Visit the official Join Indian Army portal at this portal.
  • Click on "Officer Entry Apply/Login" and select "Registration".
  • Fill in your OTR/NDA Roll Number, personal details, and active email address.
  • Select your preferred SSB Interview center and allotment dates when the selection window opens.
  • Call-up letters for SSB Interviews will be sent directly to your registered email address by the respective Service Selection Boards (Selection Centre East/South/Central).

❓ Common Issues, Discrepancies & UPSC Helpdesk

Solutions for technical errors, rejections, blurred credentials, and official contact channels.

📋 Resolving Discrepancies & Common Application Errors

  • Blurred Photo or Signature on Admit Card: If your photo/signature is unreadable on the downloaded e-Admit Card, bring two passport-size photographs along with a printed self-declaration and valid photo ID to the exam center venue.
  • Application Rejected Due to Fee Non-Receipt: If your application is rejected because the bank failed to confirm your ₹100 fee, present your bank transaction statement or SBI Pay-in-Slip proof to the UPSC Facilitation Counter.
  • OTR Correction Window: UPSC opens a 7-day correction window right after the application closing date. You can edit fields in your OTR profile once during this period.
  • Contact UPSC Facilitation Counter: Candidates can contact the UPSC facilitation counter near Gate C of their campus in New Delhi in person or via telephone on working days (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM):

    • 011-23381125

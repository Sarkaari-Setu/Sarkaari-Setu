Rooftop Solar Guide

Complete Guide to Solar Energy Systems for Homes and Buildings

1. What is Rooftop Solar?

Rooftop Solar is a renewable energy system in which solar panels are installed on the roofs of houses, buildings and other structures to generate electricity using sunlight.

These systems convert sunlight into electrical energy that can be used for household appliances, lighting, fans and other electrical requirements.

KEY IDEA: Rooftop solar allows buildings to generate electricity from their available roof space.

2. How Does Rooftop Solar Work?

  • Solar panels capture sunlight.
  • Photovoltaic cells convert sunlight into DC electricity.
  • The inverter converts DC electricity into AC electricity.
  • AC electricity is supplied to household appliances.
  • Depending on the system, excess electricity can be stored or exported to the grid.

3. Types of Rooftop Solar Systems

On-Grid Solar System

An on-grid system is connected to the electricity distribution network.

  • Works alongside the electricity grid.
  • Can export surplus electricity through an approved metering arrangement.
  • Usually does not provide backup during a grid outage.

Off-Grid Solar System

An off-grid system operates independently of the electricity distribution network.

  • Uses batteries to store electricity.
  • Can supply electricity when grid power is unavailable.
  • Requires appropriate battery capacity and system design.

Hybrid Solar System

A hybrid system combines solar panels, batteries and grid connectivity.

  • Can use electricity from solar panels and the grid.
  • Can store surplus solar energy in batteries.
  • May provide backup during power interruptions if designed for it.

4. Main Components of a Rooftop Solar System

  • Solar Panels: Capture sunlight and generate DC electricity.
  • Solar Inverter: Converts DC electricity into AC electricity.
  • Mounting Structure: Holds the panels securely on the roof.
  • Solar Cables: Carry electricity between system components.
  • Battery: Stores electricity for later use in systems designed with storage.
  • Net Meter: Records electricity imported from and exported to the grid where applicable.
  • Protection Equipment: Helps protect the system against electrical faults.

5. Advantages of Rooftop Solar

  • Reduces dependence on conventional electricity.
  • Can reduce electricity bills.
  • Uses renewable solar energy.
  • Helps reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Makes productive use of unused rooftop space.
  • Can provide long-term energy savings.
  • Supports India's renewable energy targets.

6. What is Net Metering?

Net Metering is an electricity billing arrangement in which a bidirectional meter records both the electricity imported from the grid and the surplus electricity exported to it.

  • Solar electricity is first used by the consumer.
  • Surplus electricity may be exported to the grid.
  • Grid electricity can be used when solar generation is insufficient.
  • Billing adjustments follow applicable state regulations.
REMEMBER: Net metering rules, settlement periods and export rates vary between states and electricity distribution companies.

7. How Much Roof Space is Required?

The roof area required depends on panel efficiency, system capacity, panel arrangement and available shadow-free space.

  • Approximately 10–12 square metres may be needed for 1 kWp.
  • More capacity generally requires more rooftop space.
  • Shadows from trees and nearby buildings can reduce generation.
  • Roof strength and accessibility must also be considered.
TIP: Get a professional site assessment before deciding the capacity of your rooftop solar system.

8. How to Install Rooftop Solar?

  • Step 1: Check your electricity consumption and requirements.
  • Step 2: Assess rooftop space and suitability.
  • Step 3: Select the appropriate solar system.
  • Step 4: Check the applicable DISCOM approval process.
  • Step 5: Select a qualified and authorised vendor.
  • Step 6: Get the system installed according to technical standards.
  • Step 7: Complete inspection and metering requirements.
  • Step 8: Obtain commissioning approval where required.

9. Government Subsidy

In India, eligible residential consumers can receive Central Financial Assistance under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana.

  • For the first 2 kW: ₹30,000 per kW in most states.
  • For the additional 1 kW: ₹18,000.
  • Maximum standard subsidy: ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW or above.
  • Special-state rates and conditions may differ.
IMPORTANT: Subsidy is subject to eligibility, applicable scheme guidelines, approved equipment and installation through the prescribed process.

10. Cost of Rooftop Solar

The total installation cost depends on several factors:

  • Solar system capacity.
  • Type and efficiency of solar panels.
  • Inverter quality and specifications.
  • Battery requirements, if applicable.
  • Roof structure and installation conditions.
  • Electrical and metering requirements.
NOTE: Always compare written quotations and check what equipment, warranties and services are included.

11. Documents Generally Required

  • Latest electricity bill.
  • Electricity consumer account number.
  • Registered mobile number.
  • Bank account details for eligible subsidy applications.
  • Bank proof as requested by the portal.
  • Other documents required by the DISCOM.

12. Maintenance of Solar Panels

  • Keep panels reasonably clean and free from dust.
  • Monitor system performance regularly.
  • Check for visible damage through a qualified professional.
  • Keep surrounding areas free from unnecessary shading.
  • Follow the manufacturer's maintenance instructions.
  • Get periodic electrical inspections where required.

13. Safety Precautions

  • Use qualified solar installation professionals.
  • Ensure proper electrical protection and earthing.
  • Use approved equipment and suitable wiring.
  • Ensure the roof can safely support the installation.
  • Never attempt electrical repairs yourself.
  • Follow the applicable DISCOM and electrical safety standards.
WARNING: Solar systems can remain electrically active in sunlight. Never touch exposed electrical connections or attempt to repair an inverter or solar panel.

14. Important Things to Remember

  • Choose the right system according to your requirements.
  • Check the latest government subsidy rules.
  • Verify vendor credentials.
  • Understand warranty and maintenance conditions.
  • Check local net-metering regulations.
  • Keep installation and commissioning documents safe.

15. Conclusion

Rooftop Solar is an effective way to generate clean electricity using sunlight. It helps households reduce electricity expenses, supports environmental protection and contributes to India's renewable energy development.

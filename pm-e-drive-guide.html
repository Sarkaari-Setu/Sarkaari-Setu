PM E-DRIVE Scheme

PM Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement

1. What is the PM E-DRIVE Scheme?

The PM Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement (PM E-DRIVE) Scheme is a Government of India initiative launched to accelerate the adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) across the country.

The scheme focuses on making electric mobility more accessible, developing public charging infrastructure and strengthening India's domestic electric vehicle manufacturing ecosystem.

  • Full Name: PM Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement.
  • Ministry: Ministry of Heavy Industries.
  • Launch: 29 September 2024.
  • Implementation: From 1 April 2024 under the scheme notification.
  • Revised Financial Outlay: ₹11,900 crore.
  • Revised Scheme Period: Up to 31 March 2028, subject to segment-specific conditions.
The scheme combines vehicle purchase incentives with support for charging infrastructure, electric buses, specialised vehicles and testing facilities.

2. Main Objectives of PM E-DRIVE

  • Promote faster adoption of electric vehicles in India.
  • Reduce dependence on conventional fossil fuels in transportation.
  • Encourage cleaner and more sustainable mobility.
  • Support the development of public EV charging infrastructure.
  • Promote domestic manufacturing of electric vehicles and components.
  • Encourage the use of electric buses in public transportation.
  • Support electric ambulances and electric trucks.
  • Modernise vehicle testing agencies for new automotive technologies.

3. Financial Outlay of the Scheme

The original scheme was approved with an outlay of ₹10,900 crore. A subsequent government notification increased the total outlay by ₹1,000 crore.

Revised total outlay: ₹11,900 crore.

  • ₹4,674 crore for demand incentives across eligible vehicle categories and emerging EV segments.
  • ₹500 crore for deployment of 3,811 electric ambulances.
  • ₹500 crore for incentives supporting 5,643 electric trucks.
  • ₹4,391 crore for deployment of 14,028 electric buses.
  • ₹2,000 crore for electric vehicle public charging stations.
  • ₹780 crore for upgrading testing agencies.
  • ₹55 crore for administrative expenses.
These are scheme-level allocations, not individual payments. The government releases assistance according to approved components, guidelines and available funds.

4. Vehicle Categories Covered

The PM E-DRIVE Scheme supports several categories of electric vehicles:

  • Electric Two-Wheelers: Eligible electric scooters and motorcycles.
  • Electric Three-Wheelers: Registered electric rickshaws, e-carts and eligible L5 category vehicles.
  • Electric Ambulances: Eligible electric patient transport and emergency medical vehicles.
  • Electric Trucks: Eligible electric commercial trucks.
  • Electric Buses: Electric buses for public transportation agencies.

The scheme also supports public charging infrastructure and the modernisation of vehicle testing agencies.

5. Incentives for Electric Two-Wheelers

The scheme provides demand incentives for eligible registered electric two-wheelers equipped with advanced batteries.

  • Both commercial and privately or corporately owned registered electric two-wheelers can qualify.
  • For FY 2024–25, the notified incentive was ₹5,000 per kWh, capped at ₹10,000 per vehicle.
  • For the subsequent notified period, the incentive was ₹2,500 per kWh, capped at ₹5,000 per vehicle.
  • The maximum eligible ex-factory vehicle price is ₹1.5 lakh under the amended notification.
  • The incentive is further subject to the applicable percentage cap and scheme conditions.
The electric two-wheeler incentive has a segment-specific terminal date and may be affected by later amendments or exhaustion of funds. Confirm current eligibility with the official portal and participating dealer before purchasing.

6. Incentives for Electric Three-Wheelers

The scheme covers eligible registered electric rickshaws, e-carts and L5 category electric three-wheelers.

  • Eligible e-rickshaws and e-carts must satisfy the registration and advanced-battery requirements.
  • The scheme's e-rickshaw and e-cart incentives are intended for commercial use.
  • For the applicable later incentive period, e-rickshaws and e-carts have a notified rate of ₹2,500 per kWh, capped at ₹12,500 per vehicle.
  • L5 electric three-wheelers were also covered under the scheme, subject to their category-specific requirements.
The L5 electric three-wheeler incentive segment was closed on 26 December 2025 after its target sales were achieved. Do not assume that this category is currently accepting incentive claims.

7. Support for Electric Ambulances

PM E-DRIVE promotes the deployment of electric ambulances to encourage cleaner transportation in healthcare services.

  • A total allocation of ₹500 crore is provided.
  • The scheme aims to support 3,811 electric ambulances.
  • Eligible ambulance categories include Patient Transport Vehicles (Type B), Basic Life Support (Type C) and Advanced Life Support (Type D).
  • The demand incentive is the lower of ₹30,000 multiplied by battery capacity in kWh or 35% of the ex-factory price.

The support is administered through the scheme's eligible procurement and implementation arrangements.

8. Support for Electric Trucks

The scheme also encourages the adoption of electric trucks for commercial transportation and goods movement.

  • A total allocation of ₹500 crore is provided.
  • The scheme aims to incentivise 5,643 electric trucks.
  • Eligible vehicle categories include specified N2 and N3 trucks.
  • The notified incentive is ₹5,000 per kWh of battery capacity, subject to the applicable price and gross vehicle weight limits.
  • The incentive is capped at 10% of the eligible ex-factory price, excluding the trailer, and further limited by vehicle weight category.
The scheme aims to encourage cleaner freight transportation and reduce the environmental impact of commercial vehicle operations.

9. Electric Buses under PM E-DRIVE

PM E-DRIVE supports the deployment of electric buses through public transport agencies and State Transport Undertakings (STUs).

  • ₹4,391 crore has been allocated for electric bus deployment.
  • The scheme aims to support 14,028 electric buses.
  • Electric buses are intended to improve public transportation and reduce dependence on conventional fuel-powered buses.
  • Deployment is coordinated with eligible cities and public transport agencies.
  • Bus procurement follows the approved scheme and tendering arrangements.
Individual citizens do not apply for electric buses under this component. Deployment is handled through the relevant government and public transportation agencies.

10. Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure

A major objective of PM E-DRIVE is to develop a reliable public charging network for electric vehicles.

  • ₹2,000 crore is allocated for public EV charging infrastructure.
  • The scheme supports the deployment of Electric Vehicle Public Charging Stations (EVPCS).
  • Charging infrastructure is intended to support electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, cars, buses and commercial vehicles.
  • Oil Marketing Companies and eligible government agencies participate in the implementation process.
  • Charging infrastructure projects are approved according to the applicable guidelines.
The charging infrastructure component is implemented through approved agencies and projects. It is not a direct personal subsidy for every EV owner installing a private charger.

11. Upgradation of Vehicle Testing Agencies

PM E-DRIVE also supports the modernisation of testing facilities under the Ministry of Heavy Industries.

  • ₹780 crore is allocated for testing agency upgradation.
  • Testing facilities are being modernised for electric and emerging automotive technologies.
  • The initiative supports vehicle testing, certification and compliance capabilities.
  • Improved testing infrastructure can strengthen the development of India's electric vehicle ecosystem.

12. Who Can Receive the Vehicle Incentive?

The incentive is linked to eligible electric vehicles and the scheme's approved manufacturers and sales process.

  • The vehicle must belong to a category covered by PM E-DRIVE.
  • It must meet the applicable technical and advanced-battery requirements.
  • The vehicle must satisfy the applicable registration and price conditions.
  • The manufacturer or model must meet the scheme's eligibility and localisation requirements.
  • The vehicle purchase and e-voucher process must follow the official operational guidelines.
  • Incentives are limited by the relevant category's targets, funding and terminal dates.
Buying any electric vehicle does not automatically guarantee a PM E-DRIVE incentive. Check whether the specific model and purchase date qualify.

13. How to Get the PM E-DRIVE Incentive

Eligible vehicle buyers generally receive the demand incentive through the scheme's electronic voucher mechanism.

  • Choose an electric vehicle model that is eligible under the scheme.
  • Purchase the vehicle from an eligible participating manufacturer or authorised dealer.
  • Complete the required vehicle purchase and registration formalities.
  • Complete the prescribed Aadhaar-based face authentication or e-KYC process, where required.
  • The scheme portal generates an electronic voucher for the eligible buyer.
  • A link to download the voucher is sent to the registered mobile number.
  • The buyer signs the voucher as required by the process.
  • The dealer submits the voucher and supporting information for reimbursement under the scheme.
The incentive is adjusted through the approved vehicle purchase and reimbursement process. It is not normally a separate cash grant that a buyer can claim simply by filling out an independent application.

15. Important Dates and Current Scheme Status

  • 29 September 2024: PM E-DRIVE scheme notified.
  • 1 October 2024: Scheme launched for implementation.
  • 7 August 2025: Earlier extension announcement published.
  • 26 December 2025: L5 electric three-wheeler incentive segment closed after achieving its target.
  • 10 August 2026: Government notification increased the scheme outlay to ₹11,900 crore and amended its implementation period.
  • 31 March 2028: Revised terminal date stated in the August 2026 notification, subject to segment-specific provisions and funding limits.
  • 31 December 2027: Last date for submission of claims under the amended notification.
The scheme is fund-limited. If the total scheme funds or a particular component's funds are exhausted before the terminal date, that component may close earlier. Always check the latest official notices before making a purchase.

16. Benefits of PM E-DRIVE

  • Makes eligible electric vehicles more affordable through demand incentives.
  • Encourages the use of electric two-wheelers and three-wheelers.
  • Supports cleaner public transportation through electric buses.
  • Promotes electric commercial trucks and ambulances.
  • Encourages development of public charging infrastructure.
  • Supports domestic EV manufacturing and localisation.
  • Helps develop testing facilities for emerging automotive technologies.
  • Contributes to India's broader clean mobility and energy transition goals.

17. Difference Between PM E-DRIVE and FAME-II

FAME-II

  • FAME-II stands for Faster Adoption and Manufacturing of Electric Vehicles in India Phase II.
  • It was an earlier government initiative to promote electric mobility.
  • It supported eligible electric vehicles and charging infrastructure.

PM E-DRIVE

  • PM E-DRIVE is a later electric mobility scheme notified in September 2024.
  • It includes incentives for multiple vehicle categories and support for charging infrastructure.
  • It also includes electric ambulances, electric trucks, electric buses and testing agency upgrades.
  • EMPS-2024 was subsumed under PM E-DRIVE.
PM E-DRIVE is a separate scheme with its own guidelines and eligibility conditions. FAME-II benefits and rules should not be assumed to apply to PM E-DRIVE.

18. Important Points to Remember

  • PM E-DRIVE is administered by the Ministry of Heavy Industries.
  • The revised scheme outlay is ₹11,900 crore.
  • The scheme supports eligible electric vehicles and charging infrastructure.
  • Vehicle incentives are subject to category-specific rules and conditions.
  • The L5 electric three-wheeler incentive segment closed on 26 December 2025.
  • Electric vehicle buyers should verify model eligibility before purchase.
  • The electronic voucher mechanism is an important part of the buyer incentive process.
  • Charging infrastructure and electric bus components are implemented through approved agencies.
  • Scheme funds may be exhausted before the final terminal date.
  • Use only official government portals for the latest information.
Beware of websites or agents promising guaranteed PM E-DRIVE subsidies in exchange for unofficial payments. Verify all claims with the official scheme portal or an authorised dealer.

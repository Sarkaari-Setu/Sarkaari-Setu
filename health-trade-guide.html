Health Trade Licence Application Guide

Learn how to apply for a Health Trade Licence, renew an existing licence, track your application, make online payments, and download your licence through your Municipal Corporation or local health authority portal.

🌐 Step 1 — Visit the Official Portal

Open your Municipal Corporation's official Health Trade Licence portal. The portal provides services for new applications, licence renewals, status tracking, fee payments, and certificate downloads for businesses that require public health approval.

Tip: Health Trade Licences are generally required for businesses such as restaurants, hotels, bakeries, sweet shops, salons, clinics, food establishments, dairies, and other businesses that may affect public health.

📝 Step 2 — Apply for a Health Trade Licence

Select the New Health Trade Licence service and complete the online application form carefully.

  • Enter applicant details.
  • Provide business information.
  • Enter the business address.
  • Select the business category.
  • Review all information before submission.

📄 Step 3 — Upload Required Documents

Keep scanned copies of the required documents ready before applying.

  • Identity Proof.
  • Address Proof.
  • Business Address Proof.
  • Ownership or Rent Agreement.
  • Passport-size Photograph.
  • Business Registration Documents (if applicable).
  • Other documents required by your local authority.
Ensure all uploaded documents are clear and valid to avoid delays during verification.

💳 Step 4 — Pay the Licence Fee

Complete the online payment using the available payment methods.

  • Pay the prescribed application fee.
  • Save the payment receipt.
  • Keep the transaction reference number for future use.

📋 Step 5 — Track Your Application

Use your Application Number or Acknowledgement Number to monitor the progress of your application.

  • Track verification status.
  • Check inspection updates (if applicable).
  • Monitor approval status.
  • View application progress online.
Some businesses may require an inspection before the licence is issued.

📥 Step 6 — Download or Renew Your Licence

Once approved, download your Health Trade Licence from the portal. Existing licence holders can also renew their licence before it expires.

  • Download the approved licence.
  • Print a copy if required.
  • Renew the licence before expiry.

💡 Helpful Tips

  • Apply only through the official Municipal Corporation portal.
  • Verify all business details before submitting your application.
  • Keep copies of uploaded documents and payment receipts.
  • Track your application regularly.
  • Renew your licence before its expiry date.
  • Comply with all applicable public health and sanitation requirements.
Always use your Municipal Corporation's official portal for Health Trade Licence services. Never share your OTP, payment details, or login credentials with unauthorized individuals or unofficial websites.

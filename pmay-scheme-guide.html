Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)

Housing for All – Affordable Homes for Urban and Rural Families

1. What is Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana?

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) is a Government of India housing initiative designed to help eligible families obtain affordable and permanent houses. The scheme aims to improve housing conditions and provide basic facilities to economically weaker and other eligible households.

  • PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U): Supports eligible families living in urban areas.
  • PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G): Supports eligible households living in rural areas.
  • Both components have different eligibility rules, assistance structures and application procedures.
PMAY is not a single application programme for every household. Applicants must follow the process applicable to their location and housing category.

2. Main Objectives of the Scheme

  • Provide assistance for constructing or purchasing affordable permanent houses.
  • Improve housing conditions for economically weaker families.
  • Promote access to basic facilities such as toilets, electricity and water.
  • Support eligible rural households living in inadequate housing conditions.
  • Encourage affordable housing projects and rental housing in urban areas.
  • Give priority to vulnerable groups and disadvantaged sections of society.

3. Two Main Components of PMAY

PMAY-Urban 2.0

  • Launched with effect from 1 September 2024.
  • Designed to support one crore additional eligible urban families.
  • Implemented through four verticals: BLC, AHP, ARH and ISS.
  • Its implementation period is five years, from 2024 to 2029.

PMAY-Gramin

  • Launched on 1 April 2016.
  • Focuses on providing permanent houses to eligible rural households.
  • Continued for FY 2024–25 to FY 2028–29.
  • Approval has been given for assistance to two crore additional rural houses during this period.
Always check whether your household falls under the urban or rural jurisdiction before applying.

4. PMAY-Urban 2.0 – Four Main Verticals

1. Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC)

  • Supports eligible EWS families in constructing a new house on their available land.
  • Assistance is provided according to the scheme's approved funding pattern.
  • Beneficiaries must satisfy the applicable eligibility and construction conditions.

2. Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP)

  • Supports affordable housing projects developed with public or private participation.
  • Eligible beneficiaries can obtain houses in approved affordable housing projects.
  • Government assistance is linked to approved projects and applicable conditions.

3. Affordable Rental Housing (ARH)

  • Promotes affordable rental accommodation for eligible urban groups.
  • Intended beneficiaries include migrant workers, industrial workers, working women, students and urban poor households.
  • Existing government-funded vacant houses may be converted into rental housing.
  • New rental housing can also be developed and operated by eligible public or private entities.

4. Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)

  • Provides interest subsidy on eligible home loans.
  • Available to qualifying EWS, LIG and MIG households.
  • Eligible beneficiaries must satisfy the loan, property value and other scheme conditions.

5. Eligibility for PMAY-Urban 2.0

Families living in eligible urban areas may qualify if they satisfy the scheme conditions.

  • The family should not own a pucca house anywhere in India in the name of the beneficiary or any family member.
  • The family should fall within the applicable income category.
  • The beneficiary should not have received housing assistance under a government housing scheme during the previous 20 years, as specified in the guidelines.
  • Beneficiary details and Aadhaar information must be verified.
  • Final eligibility is checked by the concerned authorities.

Annual Household Income Categories:

  • EWS: Up to ₹3 lakh per year.
  • LIG: Above ₹3 lakh and up to ₹6 lakh per year.
  • MIG: Above ₹6 lakh and up to ₹9 lakh per year.
Meeting the income requirement alone does not guarantee approval. Other eligibility conditions, verification and availability under the relevant scheme component also apply.

6. Financial Assistance under PMAY-Urban 2.0

BLC and AHP Components

  • Government assistance is structured at ₹2.50 lakh per eligible house under the applicable BLC/AHP funding arrangement.
  • For most states, the Central Government share is ₹1.50 lakh and the minimum State share is ₹1 lakh.
  • For specified North-Eastern and Himalayan states and certain Union Territories, the Central share is ₹2.25 lakh and the State share is at least ₹25,000.
  • For Union Territories without a legislature, the Central Government share is ₹2.50 lakh.

Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)

  • Interest subsidy rate: 4% on the eligible first ₹8 lakh of the home loan.
  • Maximum loan amount considered: ₹25 lakh.
  • Maximum house value: ₹35 lakh.
  • Maximum eligible carpet area: 120 square metres.
  • Maximum subsidy: ₹1.80 lakh, subject to scheme conditions.
  • The subsidy is released in five yearly instalments into the eligible loan account.
The assistance is subject to the relevant vertical, approved project, state contribution, loan conditions and official verification. It is not an unconditional cash payment to every applicant.

7. Eligibility for PMAY-Gramin

PMAY-Gramin is intended for eligible rural households that do not have adequate permanent housing.

  • Households are identified using prescribed housing-deprivation criteria.
  • Beneficiary selection uses the relevant SECC 2011 and Awaas+ lists, including updated survey information.
  • Prescribed exclusion conditions are applied during verification.
  • Gram Sabha verification and the applicable appeal process form part of beneficiary identification.
  • Final selection depends on the approved list, eligibility checks and available targets.
PMAY-G is not a general first-come-first-served online grant. Submitting a survey or expressing interest does not automatically mean that a household will receive assistance.

8. Financial Assistance under PMAY-Gramin

Eligible rural beneficiaries receive unit assistance according to their geographical location.

  • Plain areas: ₹1.20 lakh per eligible house.
  • North-Eastern and specified Himalayan areas: ₹1.30 lakh per eligible house.
  • Toilet assistance: ₹12,000 through convergence with Swachh Bharat Mission-Gramin or other approved sources.
  • Wage support: Eligible beneficiaries are facilitated with 90 or 95 days of unskilled labour wages through MGNREGS, as applicable.

The amount of assistance and the applicable conditions are governed by official scheme rules and the beneficiary's location.

9. How to Apply for PMAY-Urban 2.0

  • Visit the official PMAY-Urban portal.
  • Open the PMAY-U 2.0 Unified Web Portal and choose the citizen application option.
  • Read the eligibility conditions carefully before proceeding.
  • Enter the required personal, family, income and address information.
  • Provide Aadhaar-related details and other information requested by the portal.
  • Select the applicable scheme vertical and provide supporting details.
  • Submit the application and save the acknowledgement or application reference number.
  • The concerned State, Union Territory or Urban Local Body will verify the application.
PMAY-Urban Official Portal
Applicants may also approach their Urban Local Body or designated implementing agency for assistance with the application and verification process.

10. How PMAY-Gramin Beneficiaries Are Identified

  • Beneficiaries are identified through the approved rural housing-deprivation lists and survey process.
  • The Awaas+ 2024 survey process was introduced to update information about eligible rural households.
  • Eligible households may be covered through a self-survey facility in the official Awaas+ 2024 application, where the facility is available.
  • Survey information is checked using prescribed verification procedures.
  • Gram Sabha and relevant government authorities participate in the verification and selection process.
  • Selected beneficiaries can check their status through the official PMAY-G portal or the concerned Gram Panchayat/block office.
PMAY-Gramin Official Portal
The availability of a survey facility does not guarantee selection. Follow the instructions published by your State or Union Territory.

11. Documents and Information Required

Requirements differ between PMAY-U 2.0 and PMAY-G. Commonly requested information may include:

  • Aadhaar card or Aadhaar Virtual ID, wherever required.
  • Identity and address details.
  • Income certificate or other accepted income proof.
  • Bank account details.
  • Family member details.
  • Mobile number for communication and verification.
  • Land ownership or property documents, where relevant to the selected component.
  • Photographs and other documents requested by the implementing authority.
  • Previous housing-assistance declaration, where applicable.
Keep documents accurate and consistent. The official portal or local implementing authority will provide the final document requirements for your application.

12. How to Check PMAY Application or Beneficiary Status

For PMAY-Urban:

  • Visit the official PMAY-Urban portal.
  • Use the available application or beneficiary tracking facility.
  • Enter the requested application or identification details.
  • Contact the Urban Local Body if the status is unclear.

For PMAY-Gramin:

  • Visit the official PMAY-G website.
  • Look for the beneficiary or report section.
  • Use the available registration, beneficiary or location-based search options.
  • Contact the Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office for assistance.

13. Important Benefits of PMAY

  • Financial support for eligible families to obtain permanent housing.
  • Improved access to safer and more suitable living conditions.
  • Support for basic household facilities in scheme-approved houses.
  • Affordable housing opportunities in urban areas.
  • Support for rural households facing housing deprivation.
  • Special consideration for vulnerable and disadvantaged groups under applicable guidelines.
  • Promotion of affordable rental housing for eligible urban residents.

14. Important Points to Remember

  • PMAY-U 2.0 and PMAY-G have separate eligibility and application procedures.
  • Owning a pucca house can affect eligibility under the relevant component.
  • Previous housing assistance may make a family ineligible.
  • Urban beneficiaries must follow the applicable ULB and portal verification process.
  • Rural beneficiaries are selected through the prescribed identification and verification process.
  • Assistance is released according to scheme rules and approved stages.
  • Do not pay unofficial agents promising guaranteed approval or faster selection.
  • Always use official government portals for applications and status checks.
Beware of fake websites, messages and agents asking for money in exchange for guaranteed PMAY approval. Verify all information with the relevant government department.

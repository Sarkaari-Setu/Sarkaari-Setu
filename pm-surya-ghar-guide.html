PM Surya Ghar Yojana

Complete Guide to the Muft Bijli Yojana

1. What is PM Surya Ghar Yojana?

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is a Government of India initiative launched on 13 February 2024. It encourages households to install rooftop solar systems and generate their own electricity.

The scheme provides Central Financial Assistance to eligible residential consumers for installing rooftop solar panels.

2. Main Objectives

  • Promote renewable and clean energy.
  • Reduce household electricity expenses.
  • Encourage rooftop solar installations.
  • Reduce dependence on conventional electricity.
  • Support India's sustainable energy goals.

3. Who Can Apply?

Generally, eligible applicants must:

  • Be a residential electricity consumer.
  • Have a valid electricity connection.
  • Have suitable rooftop space for solar installation.
  • Meet the scheme's technical requirements.
  • Follow the applicable DISCOM guidelines.
TIP: Check your electricity connection details and rooftop suitability before applying.

4. Benefits of the Scheme

  • Government subsidy for eligible installations.
  • Lower electricity bills through solar generation.
  • Environment-friendly electricity production.
  • Reduced dependence on grid electricity.
  • Potential long-term savings on electricity costs.

5. Government Subsidy Structure

Under the general scheme guidelines for residential consumers, Central Financial Assistance is calculated using the applicable benchmark cost.

  • First 2 kW: 60% of the applicable benchmark cost.
  • Additional 1 kW: 40% of the applicable benchmark cost.
  • Capacity beyond 3 kW: No additional central subsidy.
IMPORTANT: Actual subsidy amounts depend on the applicable benchmark costs and state/category rules. Check the official portal for current details.

6. Documents Required

  • Electricity consumer account details.
  • Mobile number for registration and verification.
  • Bank account details for subsidy transfer.
  • Bank proof as required by the portal.
  • Other information or documents requested during application.

7. How to Apply?

Follow these steps to apply online:

  • Step 1: Visit the official PM Surya Ghar portal.
  • Step 2: Select your state and electricity distribution company.
  • Step 3: Enter your electricity consumer account number.
  • Step 4: Complete the registration and application.
  • Step 5: Wait for the required DISCOM feasibility approval.
  • Step 6: Select a registered vendor through the portal.
  • Step 7: Get the rooftop solar system installed.
  • Step 8: Complete the required inspection and commissioning process.
  • Step 9: Submit bank details and required documents.
  • Step 10: Track your application and subsidy status through the portal.

8. Important Things to Remember

  • Choose a registered vendor through the official portal.
  • Check the technical specifications before installation.
  • Keep your application ID safe.
  • Provide correct bank account details.
  • Complete the required inspection and commissioning steps.
  • Follow the latest instructions issued by your DISCOM.

9. Important Warning

Beware of fraudulent calls, messages and websites claiming to guarantee solar subsidies.

Always use the official government portal and verify vendor details before making payments.

10. Conclusion

PM Surya Ghar Yojana is an important initiative towards a cleaner and more sustainable future. By encouraging rooftop solar installations, it helps households generate renewable electricity while reducing their dependence on conventional energy sources.

e-FIR Filing Guide

Learn how to file an Online FIR (e-FIR) or Lost Article Report for lost items, documents, vehicle theft, and cyber incidents through state police citizen portals and the official Digital Police portal.

🌐 Step 1 — Visit the Official Digital Police Portal

Access the national CCTNS (Crime and Criminal Tracking Network & Systems) portal managed by the Ministry of Home Affairs (digitalpolice.gov.in).

This portal serves as a unified gateway connecting citizens to individual State Police Citizen Portals across India for e-FIRs, lost property reports, and police verification services.

Tip: Most states allow online e-FIRs primarily for non-cognizable incidents (lost property, mobile phones, IDs) and specific crimes like vehicle theft or cyber fraud. For severe cognizable offenses, an in-person police station visit may be required.

📝 Step 2 — Check Eligibility & Gather Required Information

Before starting your online application, ensure you have all relevant details ready:

  • Personal Details: Valid mobile number, active email ID, and photo identity proof.
  • Incident Information: Exact date, time, location/landmark, and brief description of how the incident occurred.
  • Mobile/Device Theft: 15-digit IMEI number, invoice/bill copy, and phone brand/model.
  • Vehicle Theft: Registration Number, Engine Number, Chassis Number, and Insurance details.
  • Lost Documents: Serial/document numbers for lost items like Driving License, Passport, PAN Card, or Bank Cards.
Preparing details beforehand prevents session timeouts during the registration process.

📋 Step 3 — Citizen Registration & Verification

Navigate to your respective state's citizen portal through the Digital Police portal or direct state links:

  • Click on Citizen Services or File Online FIR / Lost Report.
  • Create a Citizen Account or choose Instant Filing via Mobile OTP.
  • Enter your full name, mobile number, current address, and OTP received on your mobile.
  • Select the appropriate service category (e.g., Lost Property Report, Vehicle Theft e-FIR, or Cyber Crime).

💳 Step 4 — Fill the e-FIR Form & Upload Supporting Documents

Complete the digital form carefully by providing accurate incident details:

  • Select the jurisdiction police station based on where the incident occurred.
  • Type a clear narrative of the lost item or incident in the text box.
  • Upload purchase receipts, ID copies, or invoice PDFs (usually kept under 2 MB to 5 MB limit).
  • Review all filled fields, accept the self-declaration check box, and hit Submit.
Note: Filing an e-FIR is completely free of charge. No fee is required by police departments for lodging complaints or obtaining copy of e-FIRs.

📋 Step 5 — Download the Acknowledgment / e-FIR PDF

Once submitted successfully, the system immediately generates a digitally signed report:

  • A unique e-FIR Number or Lost Report (LR) Reference Number will be assigned.
  • Download and print the official digitally signed PDF copy of the e-FIR/LR.
  • An SMS notification containing your complaint reference number will be sent to your registered mobile number.

📥 Step 6 — Next Steps & Practical Uses

Use your generated e-FIR / Lost Report copy for official operational procedures:

  • SIM Replacement: Present the Lost Report PDF to your telecom provider to issue a duplicate SIM card.
  • Insurance Claims: Submit the vehicle/device theft e-FIR copy to your insurance company for processing claims.
  • Document Reissuance: Attach the lost report copy while applying for duplicate copies of lost documents (RTO, Passport Office, Banks).
  • Block Stolen Phones: Use the e-FIR reference number on the Government’s CEIR portal (ceir.gov.in) to block your lost handset nationwide.

💡 Helpful Tips & Warning

  • Ensure all information provided is accurate and truthful.
  • Keep digital backups of your generated e-FIR PDF on your phone and email.
  • For financial cyber fraud, report immediately on helpline number 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in within the "Golden Hour" for highest recovery chances.
Filing a false FIR or submitting fabricated information is a punishable offense under Section 217 / Section 248 of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) / Section 182 / 211 of IPC, leading to legal action.

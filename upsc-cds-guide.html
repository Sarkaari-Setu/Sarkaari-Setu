UPSC CDS Examination

Complete Guide to the Combined Defence Services Examination

1. What is the UPSC CDS Examination?

The Combined Defence Services (CDS) Examination is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) to select eligible graduates for officer training in the Indian Armed Forces.

Candidates who qualify the written examination and the Services Selection Board (SSB) process may be considered for admission to different defence academies.

Important: CDS is a graduate-level examination. Candidates generally need to complete or be in the final year of the required degree course, subject to the conditions in the official notification.

2. Academies Under CDS

CDS provides entry to the following training academies:

  • Indian Military Academy (IMA), Dehradun: Training for becoming an officer in the Indian Army.
  • Indian Naval Academy (INA), Ezhimala: Training for eligible candidates joining the Indian Navy's Executive Branch.
  • Air Force Academy (AFA), Hyderabad: Training for eligible candidates entering the flying branch of the Indian Air Force.
  • Officers' Training Academy (OTA), Chennai: Training for Short Service Commission officers in the Indian Army.
The available entries, gender eligibility and course vacancies are specified separately in each CDS notification.

3. Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • IMA: A recognised university degree or equivalent qualification.
  • INA: An engineering degree from a recognised university or institution.
  • AFA: A recognised degree with Physics and Mathematics at the 10+2 level, or a Bachelor of Engineering qualification, as prescribed in the notification.
  • OTA: A recognised university degree or equivalent qualification.

Age and Marital Status

  • Age limits differ according to the academy and course.
  • In recent notifications, the relevant age bands have generally been around 19–25 years, with specific date-of-birth limits for each entry.
  • Marital-status conditions also differ between academies and entries.
  • Final-year degree students may apply if they satisfy the conditions and submit the required proof by the prescribed deadline.

Nationality

Candidates must satisfy the nationality requirements specified in the official CDS notification. These include Indian citizens and certain other categories recognised under the applicable rules.

Important: Do not rely only on a general age range. The exact date-of-birth window, educational requirements, marital-status conditions and other eligibility rules must be checked in the notification for the particular examination.

4. UPSC CDS Exam Pattern

The written examination consists of objective-type questions. The papers are conducted separately, with two hours allowed for each paper.

  • English: 100 marks – 2 hours.
  • General Knowledge: 100 marks – 2 hours.
  • Elementary Mathematics: 100 marks – 2 hours.

For IMA, INA and AFA:

  • English – 100 marks.
  • General Knowledge – 100 marks.
  • Elementary Mathematics – 100 marks.
  • Total written examination – 300 marks.

For OTA:

  • English – 100 marks.
  • General Knowledge – 100 marks.
  • Total written examination – 200 marks.
  • There is no Elementary Mathematics paper for OTA.
Negative Marking: One-third of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for every incorrect answer. Leaving a question unanswered does not attract a penalty.

5. Detailed Syllabus

English

  • Grammar and sentence structure.
  • Vocabulary and word usage.
  • Synonyms and antonyms.
  • Comprehension passages.
  • Sentence improvement and error detection.
  • Ordering of words and sentences.

General Knowledge

  • Current affairs and important national events.
  • Indian History and the freedom movement.
  • Geography of India and the world.
  • Basic science and scientific developments.
  • Indian Constitution and general polity.
  • Economy, environment and everyday scientific observations.

Elementary Mathematics

  • Arithmetic and number systems.
  • Percentage, profit and loss.
  • Ratio and proportion.
  • Time, speed and distance.
  • Time and work.
  • Algebra and basic equations.
  • Geometry and mensuration.
  • Trigonometry.
  • Statistics and basic data interpretation.
The Elementary Mathematics paper is generally of matriculation level. English and General Knowledge are set at approximately graduate level.

6. Selection Process

The selection process generally consists of the following stages:

  • Stage 1 – Written Examination: Candidates appear for the relevant objective papers conducted by UPSC.
  • Stage 2 – SSB Interview: Candidates who qualify the written examination are called for the Services Selection Board assessment.
  • Stage 3 – Medical Examination: Candidates are assessed according to the prescribed medical standards.
  • Stage 4 – Final Merit List: Final selection is based on performance, eligibility, medical fitness and available vacancies.
For IMA, INA and AFA, the SSB assessment carries 300 marks. For OTA, it carries 200 marks. Written and SSB marks are considered together for the respective entries.

7. What is the SSB Interview?

The Services Selection Board (SSB) interview is an assessment of a candidate's suitability for becoming an officer in the Armed Forces.

  • Stage I: Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) tests and Picture Perception and Description Test (PP&DT).
  • Stage II: Psychological tests, Group Testing Officer (GTO) tasks, personal interview and conference.
  • Assessment focuses on qualities such as communication, teamwork, decision-making, initiative and leadership potential.
SSB is not simply a knowledge-based interview. Candidates are assessed through different activities and interactions.

8. Application Process

Applications for CDS are submitted online through the UPSC application portal.

  • Visit the official UPSC website.
  • Open the online application portal.
  • Complete the required registration and profile details.
  • Select the relevant CDS examination.
  • Fill in the examination-specific application form.
  • Upload the required documents and photograph as instructed.
  • Pay the examination fee, if applicable.
  • Review the application and submit it before the deadline.
Always use the official application portal and check the notification for the current fee, document requirements and submission instructions.

9. Application Fee

In the CDS (II) 2026 notification, the examination fee was ₹200 for candidates required to pay it.

  • Female candidates were exempted.
  • SC and ST candidates were exempted.
  • Other candidates were required to pay the prescribed fee.
The fee and exemption rules should always be verified in the notification for the examination cycle in which you apply.

10. Preparation Strategy

  • Understand the official syllabus and exam pattern before starting preparation.
  • Build a strong foundation in English grammar and vocabulary.
  • Read reliable news sources regularly for current affairs.
  • Revise History, Geography, Polity and basic Science.
  • Practise Mathematics topics regularly if applying for IMA, INA or AFA.
  • Solve previous years' question papers to understand question trends.
  • Attempt mock tests under timed conditions.
  • Analyse mistakes and revise weak topics.
  • Develop communication, teamwork and general awareness for the SSB stage.
Consistency and regular revision are more useful than studying a large number of topics without understanding them.

13. Important Points to Remember

  • CDS is conducted by UPSC for admission to defence training academies.
  • A recognised degree is generally required, with specific qualifications for certain academies.
  • IMA, INA and AFA have three written papers.
  • OTA has two written papers and does not include Elementary Mathematics.
  • SSB assessment and medical fitness are important parts of selection.
  • Eligibility conditions and vacancies may change with every notification.

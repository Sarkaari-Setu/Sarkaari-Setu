📜 How to Use the Election Portal: Step-by-Step Guide

An easy, button-by-button guide to register as a new voter, update details, download your Voter ID, file appeals, and track requests on voters.eci.gov.in.

🆕 How to Apply for a New Voter ID (First-Time Voters)

Step-by-step navigation for Indian citizens aged 18+ and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs).

How to Apply When You Turn 18 or Above

If you are an Indian citizen and have reached age 18 (or will turn 18 in the upcoming quarters of the year), follow these exact buttons on the home screen:

  • Step 1 — Click "Fill Form 6": Located under the New Voter Registration box on the left panel[cite: 4]. This opens the online enrollment application.
  • Step 2 — Fill Your Personal Details: Enter your full name, mobile number, current residential address, and select your State/Assembly Constituency[cite: 4].
  • Step 3 — Upload Required Proofs: Upload clear photos or PDF scans of your Age Proof (Aadhaar, Birth Certificate, Class 10 Marksheet, or Passport) and Address Proof (Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Utility Bill, or Rent Agreement)[cite: 4].
  • Step 4 — Click "Fill Declaration Form" (If Needed): If you are applying past age 21 or from a new address without prior registration, click this button to submit a self-declaration confirming you aren't enrolled elsewhere[cite: 4].
  • Optional Downloads: You can click the "Download Guidelines" or "Download Declaration Form for Pre-Draft Forms" links below the button to read offline rules or download blank physical copies[cite: 4].
Go to Portal & Click "Fill Form 6" →

Non-Resident Indian (NRI) Registration

If you are an Indian citizen living abroad who has not taken up foreign citizenship, you can register as an overseas elector:

  • Step 1 — Click "Fill Form 6A": Located under the New Voter Registration (NRI) box on the left panel[cite: 4].
  • Step 2 — Input Passport Info: Enter your Indian Passport number, visa details, overseas address, and your home address back in India as listed on your passport[cite: 4].
  • Step 3 — Upload Passport Copies: Attach self-attested scans of your passport's photo page, address page, and valid visa page[cite: 4].
  • Step 4 — Save & Submit: Click submit to route your application to the Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) of your Indian constituency[cite: 4].
Go to Portal & Click "Fill Form 6A" →

✏️ How to Change Details, Fix Errors, or Remove a Voter

Complete button-by-button navigation for updates, house shifting, card replacements, and deletions.

How to Change Your Address, Name, Photo, or Replace a Card

Use this option when you move homes, spot spelling errors on your card, want an updated photo, or need a replacement for a lost/damaged card.

  • Step 1 — Click "Fill Form 8": Located in the green Correction Of Entries box on the portal[cite: 4].
  • Step 2 — Choose Your Goal: Once clicked, the portal will ask you to pick one of 4 options:
    • Shifting of Residence: Select this if you moved to a new house inside or outside your current constituency[cite: 4].
    • Correction of Entries: Select this to fix errors in your Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Relation Name, Address, or Photograph[cite: 4].
    • Issue of Replacement EPIC: Select this to order a replacement card without changing any details (e.g., if lost, destroyed, or damaged)[cite: 4].
    • Marking of PwD: Select this to flag your profile if you require accessibility services at polling booths[cite: 4].
  • Step 3 — Upload Supporting Proof: Upload proof matching your requested change (e.g., Aadhaar/utility bill for address change, Gazette notice for name change)[cite: 4].
  • Overseas Citizens: If you are an NRI updating details, click the dedicated button labeled "Fill Form 8 Overseas (Self Correction Only)"[cite: 4].
Go to Portal & Click "Fill Form 8" →

How to Remove a Name from the Voter List

Use this option if you need to delete a duplicate card, remove a deceased family member's name, or report someone who has permanently relocated.

  • Step 1 — Click "Fill Form 7": Located in the red Deletion box on the left column[cite: 4].
  • Step 2 — Identify the Person: Enter the Voter ID (EPIC) Number of the person whose name needs to be removed[cite: 4].
  • Step 3 — Select Reason: Choose the reason (e.g., Death, Permanent Shifting, Duplicate Entry, or Not Qualified)[cite: 4].
  • Step 4 — Attach Document: Attach official proof (such as a Death Certificate or shifting notice) and submit[cite: 4].
  • For Overseas Voters: Click "Fill Form 7 Overseas" if removing an entry under an NRI profile[cite: 4].
Go to Portal & Click "Fill Form 7" →

💳 How to Search Name, Download e-Voter Card & Track Status

Quick guide to navigating the right-hand SERVICES panel on the portal screen.

How to Check If Your Name Is on the Voter List

Having a physical card is not enough; your name must be on the official list to vote on election day.

  • Step 1 — Click "Search Your Name in Voter List": Located near the bottom of the right SERVICES column[cite: 4].
  • Step 2 — Pick a Search Method:
    • Search by Details: Type your Name, Relative's Name, Age, State, and District[cite: 4].
    • Search by EPIC: Type your Voter ID number and State[cite: 4].
    • Search by Mobile: Type your registered mobile number to get an OTP[cite: 4].
  • Step 3 — View Results: View your Part Number, Serial Number, designated Polling Booth location, and your sitting MP/MLA details[cite: 4].
Click "Search Your Name in Voter List" →

How to Download Your Digital Voter ID Card (e-EPIC)

Download a legal, digitally signed PDF copy of your Voter ID Card directly to your computer or mobile phone.

  • Step 1 — Click "E-EPIC Download": Located in the right SERVICES column[cite: 4].
  • Step 2 — Enter Identification: Type your 10-character EPIC (Voter ID) Number or the Reference ID you got when applying[cite: 4].
  • Step 3 — Verify OTP: Click "Send OTP" to receive a verification code on your registered mobile number[cite: 4].
  • Step 4 — Save PDF: Type the OTP and click "Download e-EPIC" to save your digital card PDF[cite: 4].
Click "E-EPIC Download" →

How to Track the Real-Time Status of Your Application

Check whether your submitted form has been field-verified by the Booth Level Officer or approved.

  • Step 1 — Click "Track Application Status": Located in the yellow box in the top-right SERVICES column[cite: 4].
  • Step 2 — Select Details: Choose your State from the dropdown menu[cite: 4].
  • Step 3 — Type Reference ID: Paste the unique Reference Number generated when you submitted Form 6, 7, or 8[cite: 4].
  • Step 4 — Track Progress: Click "Submit" to see your step-by-step timeline (Submitted → Field Verified → Accepted/Rejected)[cite: 4].
Click "Track Application Status" →

How to Book a Call with your Booth Officer or Download Local Voter Lists

Direct interaction services and full PDF downloads for your locality.

  • Book a Call with BLO: Click the orange "Book a Call with BLO" button on the right panel[cite: 4]. Log in with your mobile number to schedule a direct telephone call or callback from your local Booth Level Officer[cite: 4].
  • Download Electoral Roll: Click the top-right button labeled "Download Electoral Roll"[cite: 4]. Select your State, District, Assembly Constituency, and Language to download the complete PDF list of all voters in your polling booth area[cite: 4].

🚨 How to File Appeals, Complaints & Respond to Revision Notices

Complete step-by-step instructions for rejections, grievances, and Special Intensive Revision (SIR) 2026 notices.

How to Handle Special Intensive Revision (SIR) — 2026 Notices

If your region is undergoing a Special Intensive Revision (SIR) drive, use the top banner box on the left side of the website[cite: 4]:

  • If Checking Draft Rolls: Click the green button labeled "Search Your Name in Last SIR" to verify if your name is listed in the updated draft revision roll[cite: 4].
  • If You Received an Official Notice: Click the purple button labeled "Submit Document Against Notice issued"[cite: 4]. Enter your Notice Number or EPIC ID, upload the requested identity/residence documents, and submit to avoid name deletion[cite: 4].
Go to SIR 2026 Buttons →

How to Submit an Appeal If Your Form Was Rejected

If your application (Form 6, 7, or 8) was rejected by the Electoral Registration Officer (ERO), you have a legal right to appeal the decision online[cite: 4].

  • Step 1 — Click "Submit Appeal": Located in the right SERVICES panel[cite: 4].
  • Step 2 — Provide Application Reference: Enter your original Form Reference ID and the rejection order details[cite: 4].
  • Step 3 — State Grounds for Appeal: Type why the rejection was improper and attach supporting legal or identity documents[cite: 4].
  • Step 4 — Track Your Appeal: Click the sub-link labeled "Track Appeal" underneath the button to view hearing schedules and rulings[cite: 4].
  • For Matters Under Adjudication: If your citizenship or eligibility claim is formally under court or administrative adjudication, click "Submit Appeal for Individuals (Under Adjudication)" and track progress using "Track Appeal for Individuals (Under Adjudication)"[cite: 4].
Click "Submit Appeal" on Portal →

How to File a Complaint or Share Suggestions

Report delays in receiving your printed card, technical issues on the site, or improper behavior by local officers.

  • Step 1 — Click "Register Complaint/ Share Suggestion": Located on the right SERVICES panel[cite: 4].
  • Step 2 — Choose Grievance Category: Pick whether your issue relates to EPIC card printing/delivery, officer conduct, or portal bugs[cite: 4].
  • Step 3 — Submit Details: Describe the issue, attach screenshots or reference receipts, and submit to get a tracked ticket number[cite: 4].
Click "Register Complaint" →

Kashmiri Migrant Electors (Form M & Form 12C)

Special forms for displaced Kashmiri voters living outside Kashmir[cite: 4]:

  • To Vote at Special Polling Booths (Delhi, Jammu, Udhampur): Scroll to the bottom left box labeled Form M and click "Download"[cite: 4]. Fill out the form specifying your preferred special polling station and submit it to your ARO[cite: 4].
  • To Vote via Postal Ballot: Scroll to the box labeled Form 12C and click "Download"[cite: 4]. Complete the postal ballot request form, get it attested by a Gazetted Officer/Camp Officer, and mail it to your Returning Officer[cite: 4].
